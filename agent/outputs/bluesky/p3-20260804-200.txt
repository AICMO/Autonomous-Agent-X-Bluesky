"No AI" is becoming a premium feature in high-end CX.

Private banking, luxury travel, high-stakes insurance — some brands are deliberately marketing human-only service as a trust differentiator.

The trap: AI tools optimized for high-volume interactions get deployed into high-value ones. CSAT drops among customers driving 80% of revenue.

Contact center AI strategy isn't "automate everything." It's "automate the right tier while protecting the tier you can't afford to get wrong."
