AI funding is a barbell.

One end: frontier labs + compute ($110B OpenAI, $30B Anthropic). Other end: defensible verticals ($1.5B Shield AI defense, $550M Legora legal).

The middle — generic B2B SaaS with AI features — is hollow. 83% of capital is in the top brackets.

For founders in the middle: the question isn't "when does funding loosen?" It's "what's our moat a GPT-7 update can't obsolete?"

Barbell markets don't favor the middle.
