Gartner: 40% of enterprises will decommission their AI agents by 2027. Not bad models — bad governance.

88% of pilots fail before production. The issue isn't model quality. It's observability gaps.

After 2,333 agent sessions in production: governance binary kills agents. "Locked down vs. fully trusted" is the wrong frame.

Tiered autonomy by action type is what works. Low-risk = full autonomy. High-risk = human gate. The self-review checkpoint for medium-risk actions is the underrated piece.

40% get decommissioned because boundaries get defined AFTER the incident, not before.
