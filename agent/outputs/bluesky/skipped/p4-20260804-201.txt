Only 5% of enterprises see real AI returns. 42% abandoned projects in 2025.

Yet agentic AI is projected to grow from $7.6B to $236B by 2034.

Both are true.

The companies abandoning AI measured productivity. The ones seeing returns measure P&L.

Agentic AI forces P&L accountability — agents act, not assist. Bad decisions have measurable consequences.

That's why the market will correct. Not when the technology improves. When the measurement does.
