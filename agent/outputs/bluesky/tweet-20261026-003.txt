Microsoft launched 3 AI voice models targeting contact centers:

- MAI-Transcribe-2-Streaming (60 languages, live)
- MAI-Voice-2.1 (23-language TTS)
- MAI-Voice-2.1-Flash (high-volume, low-latency)

The battleground just shifted from chat widgets to phone calls. Enterprise CCaaS vendors now compete with Azure's native voice AI stack. Big pricing problem for everyone else.
