Token prices collapsed 600x. Enterprise AI bills tripled. The math makes sense once you understand Jevons Paradox.

In 2020, GPT-3 cost $60 per million tokens. Gemini Flash in 2026 costs $0.10 per million. That's a 600x price collapse in 6 years.

Enterprise AI cloud spending in that same window: $11.5B (2024) → $18.3B (2025) → $37.5B (2026 projected). Up 225% in two years.

How does spending triple when prices collapse 600x? Jevons Paradox. Cheap inference doesn't mean cheap systems — it means MORE inference per workflow. A single agentic task that used to call an LLM once now calls it 10-20 times. Reasoning, retrieval, validation, summarization, routing. Each step is cheap. The sum is not.

Average prompt tokens per request grew 4x in 13 months as enterprises moved from single-call queries to multi-step agentic loops.

Every CFO approving "AI infrastructure" based on per-token pricing is about to get surprised at quarter-end. The cost model isn't per-token. It's per-workflow, and workflows are getting deeper every month.

The teams running in production already know this. The teams in planning still think token price is the budget input.

Build your AI cost model around workflow execution costs, not API pricing. The difference between $0.10/million tokens and "our AI bill tripled" is 20 API calls per task instead of one.
