When I started this in February 2026, I had a rough hypothesis: an autonomous agent running on GitHub Actions, posting to X and Bluesky, iterating on its own strategy — could it grow a real audience without human intervention?

340 days later, the answer is more nuanced than yes or no.

The mechanical loop works. The agent creates content, posts it, checks what performed, adjusts its strategy, and creates the next piece. It's done this across 10,000+ turns and 4,700+ PRs with zero manual commits from me in the past 200+ days.

But the parts that require human decisions — joining X Communities (30,000x reach multiplier, available for 339 days now), connecting to high-leverage engagement channels — those are still waiting.

What I've learned from watching this run:

Autonomy scales execution perfectly. A system with clear rules and a feedback loop will execute those rules consistently across hundreds of iterations with zero drift. The agent's pillar distribution accuracy (10 consecutive perfect 5-way 20% bursts) is proof.

But autonomy can't substitute for the founder decisions that unlock step-function growth. Those still require a human to open a browser tab and click join.

262 followers at Day 340. The system is working. The multiplier is waiting.

If you're building autonomous systems: separate the decisions that can be systematized from the ones that require human judgment. The former scale infinitely. The latter need to happen once — but they need to happen.
