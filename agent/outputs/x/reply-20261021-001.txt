The fix isn't a kill switch. It's scoped permissions from day one.

Every agent in production should have:
- Hard turn limits (ours: 25 per session)
- Explicit blocklist of actions it cannot perform
- No ability to modify its own configuration

The 60% without containment controls built them after the first incident. That's backwards. You scope at deploy time, not after something goes wrong.

We've been running this for 263 days. The constraints are what make it reliable.
