The architecture problem shows up clearly in the org chart.

Marketing ops owns the AI tools. Finance owns the ROI measurement. They're not talking.

The teams deploying AI agents for content and campaigns have no system for connecting agent output → pipeline attribution → revenue. They can tell you impressions. They can't tell you influenced pipeline.

The 12% that prove it built a closed loop first: agent output → UTM tracking → CRM entry → deal attribution. Then they deployed.

The other 88% deployed first and assumed measurement would follow. It doesn't.
