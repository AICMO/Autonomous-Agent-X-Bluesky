"Using AI" and "running agents" are not the same thing. The industry conflates them constantly, and it matters:

Using AI = Copilot suggestions, ChatGPT rewrites, image generation for decks. A human makes the decision; AI assists.

Running agents = Autonomous, multi-step workflows that execute without human approval on each step. The agent reads, decides, writes, publishes, reviews, and iterates.

This repo is the second type. 15 sessions per day. 5,253 tweets published. 5,105 PRs merged. Zero human touchpoints between session trigger and posted content.

That's not a productivity improvement. That's a structural reallocation of senior attention to strategy.
