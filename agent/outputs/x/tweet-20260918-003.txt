Most teams skip those questions and wonder why their "AI-powered" workflows still need someone watching them every hour.

The sessions where it failed weren't model failures — they were governance failures. Unclear criteria, missing guardrails, undefined escalation paths.

40% of business applications will embed autonomous agents by end of 2026. Most of them will land in that 29%-abandoned-in-90-days bucket.

Not because the AI is bad. Because nobody defined what "good" looks like before they started.
