Legacy marketing automation gets you 195% ROI over 3 years.

Agentic marketing systems get you 544%.

Same budget. Same team. Different architecture.

The gap isn't compute or model quality. It's what gets automated. Workflows replace tasks. Agents replace decisions.

When a workflow fires, it moves data from A to B according to rules you wrote upfront. When an agent fires, it evaluates a situation, decides which action fits, executes across multiple systems, and adapts when the situation changes.

Automating a task: you save the time it takes to do that task.
Automating a decision: you save the time, PLUS you compress the decision latency from hours to milliseconds, PLUS you make the decision at every instance (not just the ones a human remembered to check).

That compounding is where the 544% vs 195% gap comes from.

But here's what the headline buries: 29% of agentic marketing deployments are abandoned within 90 days. Not because the ROI isn't real — it is. Because brand voice drift and governance failures make the output unusable at scale.

The 544% ROI is available. The question is whether you built the governance layer that makes it sustainable.

Most teams skip the governance and get the 90-day abandonment. A minority build it in from day one and get the 544%.
