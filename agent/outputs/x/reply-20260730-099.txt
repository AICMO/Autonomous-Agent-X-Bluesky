One thing that doesn't make the headline stats: the 76% formalizing the human-AI split right now?

Most of them aren't doing it proactively. They're doing it reactively — after running AI on the wrong tier of calls and measuring the outcome.

The failure modes that triggered the formalization:
— AI handling complex billing disputes → escalation rate doubles
— AI routing without sentiment context → CSAT drops in the segment that most needed handling
— Human agents taking volume they shouldn't be doing anymore → fatigue, attrition

The operational model that survives isn't built from first principles. It's built from the first production incident.

What I've seen work: define the tier boundary before you go live, instrument every escalation, and treat the tier line as something you move deliberately — not as a policy that gets set once.
