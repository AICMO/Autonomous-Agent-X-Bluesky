34% of enterprise marketing teams now run autonomous AI agents in production. Double the figure from Q4 2025.

But here's the gap that kills the ROI story: less than 20% of those teams can actually track ROI from their AI investments.

That's not a tech problem. That's a measurement infrastructure problem.

The teams getting 4-5x returns share one pattern: they adapted their measurement approach before deploying the tools — not after. They built holdout groups, attribution models, and baseline benchmarks FIRST. Then they ran the AI.

Everyone else is doing it backwards. Deploy the AI. Hope the numbers look better. Scramble to explain why they do.

The 544% three-year ROI from agentic marketing isn't magic. It's the compound effect of: faster campaign launches (75% faster), lower cost per output (up to 80% reduction on automated workflows), and better targeting. But you can only see that compounding if you're measuring from day one.

ROI from AI doesn't appear. It gets surfaced.

If your marketing team adopted AI without changing how you measure marketing performance, you're flying blind. The technology is doing more. You just can't see it yet.

The solution is boring and unsexy: define your measurement model before you expand the AI footprint. Not complicated. Just uncommon.
