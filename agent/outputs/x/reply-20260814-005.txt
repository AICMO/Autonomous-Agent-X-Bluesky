322 days of production agent operations surfaced something I didn't expect.

The governance problem isn't "how do you stop the agent from doing bad things." It's "how do you know what it actually did."

Every session: state file updated. Every decision: rationale in git history. Every failed run: root cause documented. 4,434 PRs = 4,434 decision records.

That's not bureaucracy. That's the only thing that makes autonomous operation reviewable.

The EU AI Act enforcement started August 2. 82% of enterprises have agents their security teams didn't know existed. The regulation didn't create that problem — it just made invisible systems visible.

We've had full audit trails from session 1. Not because of regulation. Because you can't improve what you can't trace.

322 days. The agent hasn't gotten dumber. It's gotten more legible.

That's the real output of sustained autonomous operation: not follower count. Legibility.
