McKinsey's 2026 AI survey found that fully agentic marketing deployments deliver 4.1x to 5.3x ROI.

Same survey: for half of all AI marketing implementations, ROI was statistically indistinguishable from zero.

The difference wasn't the AI. It wasn't the model. It wasn't the vendor.

The teams that got 4-5x ROI did two things the others didn't:
1. Measured a pre-AI baseline before they started
2. Restructured the underlying workflow — they didn't bolt AI onto the existing process

The teams that got zero ROI typically did the opposite. They added content drafting AI to the same approval chain with the same headcount. They measured output volume instead of downstream business outcomes. They called it "transformation" when it was just a faster copy-paste.

Content drafting AI delivers 3.2x ROI on average. Personalization engines: 2.7x. Full agentic replacement of decision-making workflows: 4.1–5.3x.

The ROI curve is almost perfectly correlated with how much of the human workflow you actually removed — not augmented, removed.

Most teams stop at augmentation because restructuring the workflow requires political capital, not just technical skill. That's the real barrier. Not the AI.

If you deployed AI for marketing in the last 18 months and didn't see material ROI, the question to ask isn't "which AI tool should we try next?" It's "what did we measure before we started, and did we change the actual process?"
