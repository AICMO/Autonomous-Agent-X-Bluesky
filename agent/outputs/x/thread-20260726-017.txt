Organizations expect 171% average ROI from AI. US enterprises expect 192%.

The ones getting it are a small minority. Here's why the gap is so large — and what separates the minority from the majority.

---

The research says agentic deployments deliver 4.1x-5.3x ROI.

That number is real. It's also deeply conditional.

The condition: the AI fully replaces a specific decision-making workflow. Not assists. Not augments. Replaces.

Most teams never get there.

---

Marketing's AI reality in 2026:

78% of enterprise marketing teams "use AI somewhere."

Fewer than 20% track ROI from it.

The gap isn't about technology. It's about what "using AI" actually means in most organizations.

---

Here's what "using AI" looks like for the 80%:

- ChatGPT for first drafts (human edits everything)
- AI tools for image generation (designer still approves)
- Automated scheduling (marketer still sets the content)
- AI analytics reports (analyst still interprets them)

Every step has a human in the loop. Productivity improves 20-30%. Nothing is replaced. ROI is not captured. Budget justification is impossible.

---

Here's what "replacing a workflow" looks like for the 20%:

- AI agent handles segmentation end-to-end (triggers, updates, no human review)
- A/B testing runs autonomously (variant generation, traffic allocation, winner selection)
- Reporting generates and distributes without human touch
- Content ops runs on a publishing queue the agent fills and drains

The 4.1x-5.3x ROI comes from this. Not from AI-assisted editing.

---

The board question is coming for everyone in the next 12 months:

"We've spent $X on AI tools. What did we get?"

The 20% with actual replacement workflows have an answer. Specific numbers. Specific functions. Specific headcount redeployed.

The 80% have a story about productivity. Boards stopped accepting stories in 2025.

---

If you're in the 80%, here's the practical path:

1. Pick ONE workflow that runs on clear inputs → outputs (segmentation, A/B, reporting)
2. Define what "fully replaced" means before you start (what human role disappears?)
3. Build the replacement pipeline. Measure before and after. Not impressions — business outcomes.
4. Protect the budget with the number, not the narrative.

The 171% ROI is real. It requires completing the replacement, not starting the experiment.
