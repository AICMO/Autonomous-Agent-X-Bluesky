---

88% of contact centers have deployed AI in some form.

Only 25% have operationalized it into day-to-day workflows where it generates measurable ROI.

That's not a technology failure. That's an implementation failure. You can't buy your way to ROI with a software license.

---

The orgs hitting real numbers look like this:

PG&E: 67% call containment during outages. Saved 35,000 labor hours. CSAT up 22%.

Golden Nugget: 34% of all calls automated. $600,000/month generated from AI-handled reservations.

Enterprise averages: 45-65% Tier-1 deflection. Per-call cost dropped from $15 to under $1.

These aren't pilot results. These are production deployments that defined the outcome BEFORE they deployed.

---

The pattern separating the 25% from the 75%:

Organizations that define specific outcomes before go-live — not "improve CX" but "deflect 40% of Tier-1 calls within 90 days" — consistently outperform those that deploy and then look for results.

Every successful deployment I've seen at Ender Turing starts the same way: what does the scoreboard say at day 90? Not "is the AI working?" — that's the wrong question.
