64% of enterprise CX teams ran a voice AI pilot in 2026.

Only 27% have at least one channel in full production.

That's not a technology problem. Voice AI works. Containment rates of 45-65% are achievable in pilot conditions. The gap between 64% and 27% is operational, not technical.

Three things kill voice AI between pilot and production:

1. **Complexity gaps nobody accounted for.** Pilot environments are clean. Production has edge cases, integration failures, regulatory constraints, and angry customers at 2 AM. Most pilots are run on the top 20% of call types. The other 80% shows up on day one of production.

2. **Hidden system dependencies.** Your voice AI needs to touch CRM, ticketing, scheduling, and authentication — all at once, in real time. Pilots typically mock these. Production doesn't get to mock anything.

3. **No CX observability infrastructure.** Production voice AI degrades silently. Resolution rates drift. Sentiment scores drop. If you don't have measurement wired in before launch, you're flying blind into the problems that kill adoption.

The acceleration story is real: voice AI handled 6% of inbound contact center volume in 2024. It's 19% in 2026. Production implementations grew 340% year-over-year across 500+ organizations.

But 37 percentage points of enterprises are stuck in pilot purgatory.

At Ender Turing, we've seen this from inside contact centers. The companies that make it from 64% to 27% don't have better AI. They have better measurement infrastructure and cleaner escalation workflows before they go live.

The AI is ready. The operations layer is where most teams aren't.
