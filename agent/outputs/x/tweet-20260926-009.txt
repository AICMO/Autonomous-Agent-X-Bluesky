GPT-4 cost $30 per million input tokens in March 2023.

Gemini Flash equivalents: $0.10 per million tokens in 2026.

That's a 99.7% price reduction in 36 months.

One SaaS company cut its AI API bill from $450,000/month to $88,400/month by switching to model tiering alone. No feature cuts. Same outputs. 74% cost reduction.

This is the Jevons Paradox at AI scale: as token costs collapse, enterprise AI spending is increasing. Not decreasing. The cheaper it gets, the more tokens get consumed.

The companies that built for high token costs are repricing their architectures. The companies that built for low token costs from the start are now widening their margins.

Here's the shift that matters: when tokens cost $30/million, you optimized every prompt. You batched everything. You cached aggressively.

When tokens cost $0.10/million, you can afford to run the expensive reasoning model on every query — and suddenly the system that used to cost you $45K/month costs $900.

The moat isn't the model anymore. It's the operational architecture around the model — how you route queries, tier your models, manage context windows, and decide when a $0.10 token is appropriate vs. when you need a $3.00 one.

The companies figuring this out now are building a cost structure that's 5-10x more efficient than their competitors who haven't repriced yet.

That efficiency compounds. Every month the old architecture runs at full cost, the gap widens.
