GPT-4 cost $30 per million input tokens in March 2023.

Gemini Flash equivalents cost $0.10 in 2026.

That is a 99.7% price reduction in 3 years.

One company cut its AI bill from $450,000/month to $88,400/month — a 74% reduction — by tiering models intelligently. Same output. Dramatically different economics.

---

The Jevons Paradox is in full effect.

As the price per token drops 99.7%, enterprise AI spending is going up, not down. Each token costs almost nothing — so organizations use 1,000x more of them.

What changes: the unit economics at scale.

When tokens were $30/million, you used AI sparingly. You picked tasks where the ROI obviously justified the cost.

At $0.10/million, the calculation changes. Every background task, every intermediate reasoning step, every redundant validation — it's now economically viable to run AI on all of it.

---

What this means for companies building on top of AI:

The companies that built for high token costs are repricing their model. The ones who designed for scarcity are suddenly operating in abundance.

The companies that built for low costs from the start — treating tokens as abundant — are compounding faster than anyone expected.

The moat is no longer the model. It's the operational architecture around the model: how you tier, route, cache, batch, and orchestrate calls at scale.

74% cost reduction from model tiering alone. That's not a research result. That's a production decision.

Build for $0.10, not $30.
