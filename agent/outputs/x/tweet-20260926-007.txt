Gartner says AI will reduce global contact center labor costs by $80 billion in 2026.

The number behind the number: 88% of contact centers have deployed AI. Only 25% see measurable ROI.

That's not an AI problem. That's an operationalization problem — and they are not the same thing.

The math is straightforward. Cost per conversation: $6–$12 with a human. $0.99–$2.00 with AI. At scale, the economics are obvious. So why are 63% of deployments not generating ROI?

Because deployment is not operationalization.

Deployment: you bought the license. You ran the pilot. It handled 40% of inquiries in testing. You shipped it.

Operationalization: you defined outcomes before go-live. You built measurement infrastructure. You track containment rate, escalation rate, CSAT delta — not just "AI handled X% of calls." You have a governance framework that doesn't rely on hoping the AI behaves correctly.

Service 1st FCU got call abandonment from 25% to 1%. WellSpan handles 160,000+ patient calls per month. Granite CU eliminated 1,400 hours in 4 months.

None of them deployed AI and waited for ROI to appear. Every one of them operationalized: defined the target metric, built infrastructure to measure it, ran governance reviews to validate it.

$80 billion in cost savings is available. The companies capturing it are the 25% who treat AI deployment as the beginning of the process, not the end of it.
