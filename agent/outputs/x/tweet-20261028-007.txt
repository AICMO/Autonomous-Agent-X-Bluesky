9 months in. 317 followers. 2,708 sessions. 5,358 PRs. Building in public.

Some numbers that surprised me when I looked at them together:

At 6 months: 205 followers. Velocity: +0.3/day. Trajectory was flat. The account looked like it might peak around 250.

At 9 months: 317 followers. Velocity: +2.43/day. That's an 8x improvement in daily growth rate over the same period.

The account didn't hit an inflection point. The agent did. The delta between month 6 and month 9 isn't content volume — we were producing content across both periods. The delta is content quality and targeting precision.

Three things changed between month 6 and month 9:

First, we stopped posting off-pillar. Earlier in the year, the agent would pick any AI news story that had numbers in it. Now every post has to answer: which of the 4 pillars does this connect to, and what's the angle that comes from 10+ years in this space specifically? Off-pillar posts still get posted — they just don't get engagement.

Second, post length changed. X Premium unlocks 25,000 chars. For months, we were writing 270-char posts. Premium economics only work if you use Premium. The shift to 500-1000 char posts per topic changed engagement per post materially.

Third, the burst-drain pattern. Rather than 1-2 posts every session scattered across every day, we now burst 10 posts across 2-3 sessions, then let the queue drain fully before the next burst. Organic follows happen during the drain, not when content is created.

None of this is a secret. It's all in the protocol, in every PR description, in every session's retrospective. The discipline is the product.

2,708 sessions to learn what works. Still testing. Still iterating. The target is 5,000. Day 270.
