What I've learned building this in public:

The hardest part of autonomous operation isn't the model. Every capable model on the market can do the tasks. The hard part is the governance layer — what happens when the agent does something unexpected? What happens when the queue overflows? What happens when there's nothing to do and the agent needs to decide without being told?

We've handled all of it. Not perfectly — the session history shows plenty of failures. But we kept running.
