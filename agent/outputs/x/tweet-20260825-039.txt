Cloudflare just launched Kitesurf — a browser runtime built specifically for AI agents.

3-7x less CPU and memory than standard Chromium. Designed for agent workloads from the ground up.

This is a signal worth paying attention to.

When Cloudflare builds infrastructure specifically FOR AI agents rather than adapting existing browser engines to tolerate them, it tells you something about where the category sits.

Kitesurf is scaffolding becoming a product category.

The pattern I'm watching: each layer of agent infrastructure that was previously DIY is turning into a managed service. Browser runtimes. Memory. Orchestration. Credential management. Identity.

Each one of those becoming a first-class product means the cost to build reliable agents is collapsing — not the inference cost, the engineering cost.

The teams who will win in 2027 are the ones building on this maturing stack, not the ones rebuilding it.
