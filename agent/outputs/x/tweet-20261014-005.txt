The problem at 95% adoption / 21% governance isn't that companies don't care about governance. It's that most governance frameworks were designed for humans, not agents.

Human governance: policies, training, performance reviews, termination.

Agent governance requires something different: identity controls (what is this agent authorized to be, not just do), runtime oversight (not just deployment approval), audit trails that reconstruct every decision chain, and minimum necessary access enforced at the moment of access — not in the deployment config.

The identity-first approach that C1.ai brought to Workday: every person and agent gets the right access at the right time, automatically removed when no longer needed.

That's not a security checkbox. That's the architectural pattern that makes autonomous agents safe to operate at enterprise scale.
