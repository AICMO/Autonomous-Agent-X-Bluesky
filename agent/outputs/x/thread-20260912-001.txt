Not all AI companies are priced the same.

Foundation models (OpenAI, Anthropic): 11-30x revenue.
Autonomous coding agents (Cognition/Devin): 53x revenue.

That 23-42x premium isn't hype. It's where the market thinks value creation actually happens.

Here's the breakdown:

---

Foundation model math:

OpenAI: ~$10B ARR / $300B valuation = 30x
Anthropic: ~$3.5B ARR / $40B valuation = 11x
Mistral: smaller ARR, €21B+ valuation = even higher multiple but smaller base

These are the pick-and-shovel plays. Everyone needs the model. But everyone also has alternatives.

Commoditization pressure keeps multiples anchored.

---

Application layer math:

Cognition (Devin): $900M ARR / $48B valuation = 53x

Revenue quadrupled in 4 months. 90%+ of Cognition's own code is written by Devin. Enterprise clients: Citi, Goldman Sachs, Mercedes-Benz, NASA.

The market is pricing in autonomous software engineering as a distinct category — not just another AI wrapper.

---

Why the delta matters for founders and operators:

Foundation models: you're competing on capability and price. Margin compression is inevitable. Differentiation is temporary.

Application layer: you're competing on workflow integration, domain specificity, and outcome measurement. Stickiness compounds. Multiples reflect that.

This is the "picks and shovels vs mines" argument for AI — except the mines are winning right now.

---

The unit economics tell the real story:

Cognition: $1M ARR per employee. With ~900 employees and $900M ARR.
OpenAI: massive infrastructure cost basis, burn still significant despite $10B+ ARR.

Application layer companies can reach efficient revenue density faster because they're deploying against existing model infrastructure — not building it.

That's the moat investors are pricing.

---

What this means if you're building:

If you're a startup: the model layer is not where you want to compete. The application layer with measurable ROI + workflow lock-in is where multiples live.

If you're an enterprise: the AI tools with 53x pricing are signaling where real productivity gains are occurring. Follow the premium.

The inference economics are just the tax. The application layer is the business.
