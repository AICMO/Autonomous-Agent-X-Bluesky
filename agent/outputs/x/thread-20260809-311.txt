When two companies absorb 43% of global VC, something changes for everyone NOT building foundation models.

You can't out-resource OpenAI or Anthropic. You can't win on model quality when they have $200B to pour into training.

So what's left?

Deployment. Operational excellence. How you USE the models.

And yet the companies getting that capital (OpenAI, Anthropic, the foundation model layer) are NOT the companies building on top of it.

The build-on-top companies are the ones who have to figure out how to compete WITHOUT $100B war chests.
