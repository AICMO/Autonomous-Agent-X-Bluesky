**The failure mode I see most often:**

Organizations deploy voice AI and measure containment rate. Wrong metric.

Containment tells you what the AI handled. It doesn't tell you what happened to revenue.

The correct metrics for a voice AI deployment:
1. Revenue per contact (are deflected calls creating or destroying revenue?)
2. Escalation reason classification (why are calls transferring to humans?)
3. Resolution rate at first touch (not just "did the AI answer" but "did the customer leave with what they needed")

Organizations that measure containment as the primary KPI are measuring what's convenient, not what matters.
