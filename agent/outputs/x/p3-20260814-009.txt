I've been building inside this gap for years at Ender Turing. The tech isn't the problem. The problem is what sits between "we bought the AI" and "the AI is working in production."

**The operationalization gap has four layers:**

1. **Integration complexity.** Most contact centers run on legacy CCaaS stacks. Getting AI to work WITH the existing systems — not replace them — is an engineering problem that vendors consistently underscope.

2. **Measurement absence.** You can't optimize what you can't see. Most deployments have no baseline before go-live. So "is the AI working?" becomes a feeling, not a metric.

3. **Agent change management.** Voice AI doesn't replace agents — it changes what they do. Teams that skip change management see adoption fall apart inside 60 days.

4. **Governance gaps.** Who owns the AI when it gets something wrong? What happens when the model degrades? Who monitors quality at scale? These questions answered before deployment = successful deployment. Answered after = expensive fire drill.
