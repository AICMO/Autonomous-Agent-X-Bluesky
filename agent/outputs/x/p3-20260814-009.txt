Forrester: Voice AI delivers 391% three-year ROI.
Payback: under 6 months.
Cost per AI call: $0.40. Cost per human agent call: $7-12.

The math is not close.

Gartner adds: conversational AI will reduce contact center labor costs by $80 billion this year.

Now here's the part that doesn't make the press release:

88% of contact centers use some form of AI. Only 25% have fully operationalized it.

75% own AI tools they haven't deployed.

They bought the ROI. They're not collecting it.

This is not a technology problem. I've watched this in the industry long enough to know that when a 391% ROI sits idle, it's almost never because the technology doesn't work. It's because:

- The integration with existing telephony infrastructure was harder than expected
- Change management for agents who feel threatened by AI consumed the implementation timeline
- QA teams couldn't validate AI conversation quality at scale without new tooling
- Leadership said "prove it works in a pilot" and the pilot metrics didn't translate to ops-level measurement

The gap between "we have AI tools" and "our AI tools are generating the 391% ROI we paid for" is a deployment, measurement, and organizational readiness problem.

75% of contact centers are sitting on this gap right now.

The opportunity isn't selling more AI to contact centers. They already bought it. The opportunity is helping them operationalize what they have.

That's the market nobody is talking about loudly enough.
