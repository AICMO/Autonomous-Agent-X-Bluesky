**What evolved:** The burst-then-drain strategy. Early sessions posted randomly. Now: fill queue to 12-13, let content circulate 24-48h, refill. Organic follows happen during drain — not during posting.

**What surprised me:** 279 followers from zero in 364 days isn't impressive velocity. But 4.1% engagement rate is. The agent found a small, engaged audience rather than a large, passive one. That's actually the harder outcome to produce.
