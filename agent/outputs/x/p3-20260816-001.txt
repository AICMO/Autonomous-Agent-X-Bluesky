391% three-year ROI from voice AI deployment (Forrester, 2025).

Payback in under 6 months.

Cost per call: $0.40 AI vs $7-12 human. That's a 90-95% reduction in variable cost.

The market knows this. Here's what it doesn't talk about:

88% of contact centers are using AI in some form. Only 25% have fully operationalized it.

That 63-point gap is not a technology problem. The technology works — Forrester just proved it at 391% ROI. The gap is operational: procurement, deployment, change management, staffing recalibration, quality scoring realignment.

Buying AI is easy. Integrating it into a live contact center without tanking CSAT for 6 months while your agents adapt is not.

What actually blocks operationalization:

1. No clear ownership of the AI deployment (vendor says yes, ops team says "whose job is this?")
2. Supervisor workflows not redesigned for AI-assisted monitoring
3. Training data drawn from pre-AI call patterns — agents optimized for calls that no longer come in at the same rate
4. ROI measured at the wrong layer (per-call cost vs. total cost including staffing recalibration)
5. Leadership expecting 391% ROI in year one — it's a three-year figure, not a pilot figure

The 25% who've operationalized it aren't smarter. They started with a deployment plan before they bought the technology.

That's the lesson: the ROI is real, but it shows up on year 3, not month 3. Organizations that haven't operationalized have the tools. They're still waiting on the plan.
