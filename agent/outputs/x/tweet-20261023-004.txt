Forrester Consulting measured the 3-year ROI on voice AI in contact centers: 331–391%. Payback period under 6 months. One company in the study: $10.3 million saved over 3 years from a deployment that cost less than a year of savings to recoup.

These are not pilot numbers. These are production deployments.

What changed to make this math work in 2026 vs. 2023:

1. Call quality is production-ready. Early voice AI had a 15-20% misrecognition rate in real contact center audio — overlapping speech, background noise, accents, domain vocabulary. Today it's 2-4% in comparable conditions. That's the line between "useful tool" and "deployable system."

2. Handoff protocols are standardized. The failure mode in 2022-2024 wasn't the AI handling calls — it was the transfer. Agent-to-human handoffs that lost context, repeated the caller, and restarted authentication. That's solved now in every production platform worth naming.

3. Integration depth. 2023 deployments often ran parallel to CRM — voice AI handled the call, human updated the record. 2026 deployments write directly: CRM updates, follow-up scheduling, QA scoring, coaching flag — all from the same call, automatically.

The 331% ROI isn't from the AI alone. It's from eliminating the 3-5 manual steps that used to happen after every call.

At Ender Turing, we measure this layer obsessively. The ROI isn't in the call — it's in what happens to the data the call generates.
