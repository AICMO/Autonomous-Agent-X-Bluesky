Here's what gets missed in every voice AI deck: the cost per call number is only half the story. The other half is call volume. AI doesn't just replace human calls at lower cost — it handles call volume that was previously abandoned or never attempted. 20-30% more total call volume at 50-100x lower cost per interaction.

The enterprises hitting the high ROI numbers aren't doing 1:1 replacement. They're handling demand that didn't exist before because it was too expensive to serve.

McKinsey analysis: 40-50% headcount reduction while handling 20-30% more total call volume. That math doesn't work with straight replacement. It works when AI serves the demand that human capacity never could.

Call center economics aren't being disrupted. They're being inverted.
