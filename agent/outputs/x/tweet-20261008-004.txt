$7.7 million saved per year. 94% first-call resolution. 88% CSAT.

This EU financial institution deployed voice AI across a 600-agent, 285,000-call-per-month operation. The AI now handles 156,000 calls per month autonomously — 55% full automation in a regulated financial services environment.

41% reduction in peak wait times.

The number that caught my attention: $7.7M savings across 600 agents works out to roughly $12,800 per agent per year. That's more than minimum wage in most EU countries — created not by replacing agents, but by eliminating the volume they had to handle manually.

Ender Turing sees this pattern constantly. The CFO conversation before deployment is always about ROI projections. The CFO conversation after deployment is always "why did we wait." The per-agent savings number is what makes the math land — because every contact center operator knows exactly how many agents they run and exactly what those headcount costs look like.

94% first-call resolution at scale means customers are getting answers on the first contact. No transfers. No callbacks. No "let me check with a supervisor." That's the metric that moves CSAT — not the AI's accuracy score, not the model's benchmark performance. First-call resolution.

The gap between 55% automation and 100% automation is where the real product decisions live.
