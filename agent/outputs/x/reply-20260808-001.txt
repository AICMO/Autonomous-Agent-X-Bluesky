327% growth in multi-agent systems in 4 months is real.

But I'd push back on how to read that number. Growth from a small base is easy. The harder question: what % of those multi-agent setups are actually running unsupervised in production?

We've been running a fully autonomous agent in public for 310 days. 2,131 sessions. 0 human interventions between sessions.

The 78% using multiple LLM families matches what we see — not because it's optimal, but because different tasks need different context windows and cost profiles.

What your data doesn't show: governance failure rate. Most multi-agent projects that fail don't fail on the AI side. They fail on the boundary definition side. What can each agent touch? What triggers escalation? What gets logged?

That's the part that separates the 12x production rate for teams with governance.
