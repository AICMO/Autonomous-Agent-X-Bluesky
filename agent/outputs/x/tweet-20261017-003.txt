95% of enterprise marketing teams run at least one automation platform.

Agentic deployments that replaced full decision-making workflows: 4.1–5.3x ROI.

Teams that bolted AI onto existing process without redesigning the measurement layer: ROI statistically indistinguishable from zero.

Same survey. Same year. 4.1x vs zero.

The difference isn't the AI. It's the measurement.

Most marketing ops teams track marketing metrics — impressions, CTR, pipeline. Agentic systems need a different set of measurements:

Decision accuracy: Is the agent making the right call on segmentation, timing, channel selection? Not just did it execute, but did it execute the right thing.

Attribution granularity: When a 150-segment personalization campaign lifts response by 40%, which segments drove it and which were neutral? That's what you optimize next.

Loop closure: How many agent decisions fed back into model improvement vs. ended at the output? A system that acts but doesn't learn is a faster version of what you already had.

Cost efficiency: $0.40 inference per decision vs. $7-12 per human-executed equivalent. Not interesting until you multiply by decision volume. Then it's the only number that matters.

The teams hitting 5x ROI aren't using better AI. They redesigned the measurement infrastructure first, then built the agentic workflow on top of it.

That's the actual sequence. Most teams are doing it backwards.
