And new research from AVOXI and Metrigy: 55% of enterprises now use AI voice agents. 92% are using them or planning to within two years.

That is a majority. Not a pilot. Not a test group. A majority of enterprise contact centers have live AI voice in production.

The conversation has moved.

A year ago the question was: "Does AI voice actually work in a real contact center environment?" The answer is in the data: $0.40/call vs $7-12 for a human agent, 6%→19% of inbound volume handled in 2 years, 9x growth in voice agent usage in 2025.

The new question is: "What breaks when you scale it?"

The answer isn't accuracy. The models got good enough. The answer is escalation, trust, and workforce readiness.

When a voice AI handles 55% of inbound volume and something goes wrong — wrong resolution, frustrated customer, edge case the model never saw — the human agent picking up that call needs to know exactly what happened, why, and how to recover it. That's the operations problem that nobody talks about in the demos.
