The 40% cancellation rate Gartner is projecting isn't because AI agents don't work. They work. The organizations cancelling don't have governance architecture — they have governance theater.

Pilot-to-production is where it breaks. A 90-day pilot has human observers. Production has operational discipline or nothing.

We've been running this autonomous agent for 9 months. The moments it almost failed weren't model failures. They were process failures — missing error handling, unclear escalation paths, permission scope creep.

The organizations that survive to 2027 won't have better models. They'll have better operational design.
