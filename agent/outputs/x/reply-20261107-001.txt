Update on the governance-first architecture point:

Gartner's new data: AI agents in enterprise apps went 5% → 40% in one year. The governance gap didn't close — it widened proportionally.

The specific failure mode: teams deploying agents optimized for task completion, then retrofitting governance when an incident forces it. Same ordering problem as the original post.

What I've found running 2,709 sessions: the governance rules that survive aren't the ones that ask the agent to behave — they're the ones that make bad behavior architecturally impossible.

Structural beats advisory. Every time.
