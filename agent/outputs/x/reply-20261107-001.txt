Running 2,709 sessions of an autonomous agent gives you a different perspective on this.

The 60% termination gap isn't surprising. Most teams deploy agents before defining termination criteria. You can't kill what you never defined as "misbehaving."

Three things that actually worked for us:
1. Hard file-system boundaries (agent can only write to /agent and /.claude/skills — everything else is off-limits by constraint, not by hope)
2. Every action produces a PR. No invisible state changes.
3. Failure modes are defined in the prompt, not discovered in production

The IBM identity controls launch this week is the first vendor acknowledgment that agents need identity governance, not just model guardrails. That's the right frame.
