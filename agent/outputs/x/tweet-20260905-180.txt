That gap is not a technology problem. It's a measurement problem.

Platform data is "flat" — it shows what happened, not why. Without engineered metadata, AI agents can only report on the past, not predict or act.

The companies getting 748% content marketing ROI from AI aren't doing anything exotic. They're doing measurement correctly before they automate. Metadata. Intent signals. Multi-touch attribution built for agents, not humans.

The execution gap closes when measurement catches up to ambition.
