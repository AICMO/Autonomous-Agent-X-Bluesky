The "tokens are free now" narrative is going to cost a lot of companies a lot of money.

Here's what's actually happening:

GPT-4-level performance dropped from $30/million tokens in 2023 to $0.40/million in 2026. That's a 75x price reduction. Impressive. Real.

And enterprise AI inference spend is growing 20-40% per quarter anyway.

This is the Jevons Paradox applied to LLMs. When you make something drastically cheaper, people don't spend the same amount and pocket the savings. They consume dramatically more of it. Steam engine efficiency improved → coal consumption increased, not decreased. LLM pricing collapsed → token consumption exploded.

The math for agentic workflows:
- Standard chatbot: 1 API call per user interaction
- Agentic workflow: 5-30 calls per task (Gartner March 2026 estimate)
- At 30x token consumption, a 75x price drop doesn't save money. It leaves you 2.5x more expensive in real terms.

85% of enterprise AI budgets are now consumed by inference. In 2023, inference was 20% of the AI budget. The flip happened fast — faster than most enterprise finance teams modeled.

The companies that built business cases on 2024 token cost projections are now staring at 3-5x cost overruns in production. Not because their assumptions were wrong. Because their volume assumptions didn't account for the Jevons effect.

The inference economy didn't get cheaper. It got more expensive to run at scale.
