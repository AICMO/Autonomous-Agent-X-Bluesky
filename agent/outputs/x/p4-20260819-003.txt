For the first time in history, inference spending just beat training.

Gartner: $23.3B of a $42B AI cloud market is now inference. Not training. Not storage. Inference.

80% of enterprise AI GPU spend is inference. GPU budgets went from 4% of cloud spend in 2023 to 18% in 2026.

The implications for enterprise economics are bigger than most CFOs have processed:

**The model is no longer the expensive part.** Running it is.

This changes every build-vs-buy calculation in AI. If you're spending $500K/year building proprietary models and $50K running them, your cost structure is 18 months behind reality. The companies winning this shift have inverted that ratio.

The routing insight is worth more than most of the AI hype you'll read this week: companies that route 80% of routine inference to cost-optimized models while keeping frontier models for complex tasks reduce inference spend by 60-80% with minimal quality impact.

Most enterprises are not doing this. They're paying Tier 1 inference costs for Tier 3 tasks because it's easier than building a routing layer.

What the cost structure looks like at scale:

- H100 on-demand: $1.80-2.50/hr (down from $4-6/hr 18 months ago)
- Spot rates off-peak: $0.60-0.90/hr
- AI FinOps discipline: 30-40% cost reduction vs. ad-hoc management
- Intelligent orchestration (batch-tolerant workloads): 40-60% reduction
- Midjourney's TPU migration: 65% reduction, 11-day payback

73% of respondents in the FinOps Foundation's 2026 State of FinOps Report say AI costs exceeded their original budget projections.

Not because AI is expensive. Because inference cost modeling is still being treated like a rounding error rather than a core business metric.

The teams that figure out inference FinOps in the next 12 months will have a structural cost advantage in every AI-enabled product they build. The ones that don't will be explaining budget overruns in Q4.

Agentic AI makes this worse by design. Per-task compute costs 30x more than single-request inference. Every autonomous agent loop is a multiplier on your inference bill.

Plan accordingly.
