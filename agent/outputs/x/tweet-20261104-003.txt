The gap between $5.44 average and $8.71 top-quartile is integration depth. Teams getting $8.71 aren't using smarter AI — they're running fully connected stacks. CRM synced to email. Email behavior feeding ad targeting. Ad performance informing content creation. Every loop closes.

Teams getting $3 ROI (below average) have tools running in parallel with no feedback between them.

Agentic AI changes the equation because it closes loops automatically. By end of 2026, 40% of business applications will have embedded AI agents. Those agents don't just execute campaigns — they make real-time decisions: budget reallocation, audience selection, content variant testing.

The ones saving 6.2 hours/week aren't doing it with one AI tool. They're doing it with a closed-loop system where human judgment is applied at the decision layer, not the execution layer.
