The three things that separate the 5% that ship from the 95% that don't:

1. Decision boundary — written down before deployment, not invented after the first incident. What can the agent do without human approval? Be specific.

2. Audit trail — not logs. Replays. Can you reconstruct exactly what happened and why? If not, you can't improve it.

3. Drift signal — how do you know when the agent is underperforming before users tell you? What's your quality baseline?

None of this is hard. All of it gets skipped when the demo works and the pressure to ship is on.
