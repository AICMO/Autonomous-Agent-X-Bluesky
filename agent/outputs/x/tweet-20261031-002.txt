Inference costs dropped 81.3% since March 2023. Silicon Data's LLM Token Expenditure Index hit $0.97/million tokens last month.

Here's the part nobody's talking about: inference is now 70-80% of GPU cloud spend for production AI teams.

The cost collapse didn't reduce the problem. It moved it.

When tokens cost $60/million (2023), teams built narrow, conservative pipelines. Limited calls, guarded architecture, tight budgets. The constraint forced discipline.

Now tokens cost $0.97/million. Teams build sprawling multi-agent workflows. 40 tool calls per task. Reasoning loops running in parallel. Frontier models at $15-25/million output tokens for the "important" decisions.

The budget explodes upward even as the per-token cost collapses. Enterprise AI budgets went from $1.2M/year (2024) to $7M/year today. 5.8x in 2 years.

The inference cost problem is solved. The inference spend problem is getting worse.

Self-hosted inference (Spheron H100) runs ~$1.90/million tokens vs AWS at $5.46/million. The 3x gap between cloud and self-hosted is now worth engineering. That math didn't work at $0.40 when the infra investment would take years to amortize.

The economics of AI infrastructure changed twice in 18 months. Most teams optimized for the first change (cheaper tokens). Most haven't adjusted for the second (inference now dominates total cost).
