AI inference cost collapsed 150x since 2021.

GPT-4-equivalent: $60/M tokens in November 2021. $0.40/M tokens in May 2026.

Silicon Data's LLM Token Expenditure Index confirms: $0.97/M tokens as of September 2026. 81.3% drop from March 2023 alone.

Here's the part nobody talks about at the headline level:

Inference is now 70-80% of total GPU cloud spend for production AI teams.

The cost collapse didn't reduce the inference problem. It redistributed it. Prices dropped 150x. Consumption went up 100x. The cost optimization problem didn't disappear — it migrated from "can we afford to run this model?" to "how do we control our inference footprint at scale?"

Self-hosted H100 (Spheron): ~$1.90/M tokens
AWS managed: $5.46/M tokens
Frontier reasoning models: $15-25/M output tokens
GPT-5.4: $2.50 input / $15 output per M tokens

AI startup gross margins: 45% in 2025, projected 53% in 2026. Traditional software: 60-80%. The gap hasn't closed. It narrowed slightly.

The 150x cost drop is real. The inference margin problem is also real. These two facts coexist.

If you're building an AI product and your unit economics depend on inference costs continuing to fall at the same rate — recheck that assumption. The 2021-2026 curve was exceptional. The next 5 years won't look like the last 5.

What this means for teams building now: optimize for inference efficiency from architecture design, not after your cloud bill shows up.
