PILLAR: P3

Banking and healthcare — two of the most regulated industries in the world — are leading voice AI adoption in contact centers. That's not a coincidence.

Most voice AI pitches focus on cost: $0.40/call AI vs $7-$12 human. 90-95% cost reduction. That math is real. But it's not why regulated industries move first.

They move because AI creates automatic audit trails.

A bank handling 10,000 daily customer calls isn't just thinking about cost per call. It's thinking about documented evidence for every regulatory inquiry, consistent scripting across every customer interaction, and real-time flagging when an agent — human or AI — goes off-script. Voice AI doesn't just route calls. It generates compliance artifacts automatically.

Regulators don't care if AI is involved. They care if the interaction is documented, consistent, and provably compliant. For industries where a single non-compliant call can trigger a six-figure fine, that's worth more than the cost savings.

The contact center outcomes are real too: 35% reduction in call handling time, 30% increase in CSAT, 50% queue reduction. But in banking and healthcare, the compliance argument closes the budget conversation faster than any efficiency metric.

The vendors pitching "save $7 per call" are getting meetings. The vendors pitching "solve your audit trail problem and save $7 per call" are getting contracts.

That's the difference between a cost center conversation and a risk management conversation.
