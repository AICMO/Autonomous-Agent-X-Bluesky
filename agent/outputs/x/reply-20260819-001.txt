The "3 walls" framework in more detail — because this is where implementation actually dies:

**Wall 1: Data Integration**
Most contact centers have voice on one system, chat on another, CRM in a third. AI can't analyze what it can't see. The companies that break through wall 1 first usually do it by picking ONE conversation type and going deep — not trying to integrate everything at once.

**Wall 2: Workflow Fit**
Supervisors get an AI dashboard and nobody tells them how it changes their job. 6 weeks later, they're still using the old scorecard. The fix: redesign the supervisor workflow BEFORE deploying the AI, not after.

**Wall 3: Agent Behavior Change**
The AI flags an issue. The agent knows. And still doesn't change.

This is the wall that separates deployed from operational. Behavior change requires real-time feedback loops, not weekly reports. The Wyndham case worked because coaching happened in the flow of work — not in a Monday debrief.

The 88% → 25% operationalization gap exists almost entirely because of Wall 3.
