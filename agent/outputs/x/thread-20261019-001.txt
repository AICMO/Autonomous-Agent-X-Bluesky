Voice AI now handles 19% of inbound contact center volume. Up from 6% two years ago.

$0.40 per call vs $7–12 for a human agent. 3x growth. The math is brutal and obvious.

But 75% of contact centers running voice AI are still in "limited deployment." The technology works. The operations layer is what's breaking.

Here's what nobody's writing about: what actually happens after you flip the switch.

---

You deploy a voice AI. First 30 days look great. Containment rate hits 60%. Finance team cheers. Leadership declares victory.

Then month 2:

- Edge cases the AI wasn't trained on start surfacing
- Customers escalate frustrated that the AI "doesn't understand"
- Agents who DO get escalations start receiving harder, angrier calls
- After-call work spikes because the handoff notes from the AI are incomplete
- QA team has no framework for evaluating AI-handled interactions

The voice AI didn't fail. The operations layer wasn't ready.

---

The operations layer is 5 things:

1. **Handoff protocol** — how the AI passes context to a human. Not "here's the transcript." Structured: intent confirmed, data collected, next step recommended. If an agent has to re-read 3 minutes of conversation log to pick up where the AI left off, you've added friction, not removed it.

2. **CRM write-back** — every AI interaction must create a record. Disposition code. Duration. Resolution type. The AI call that doesn't write to CRM is invisible to your analytics. Invisible = unmeasured = unmanaged.

3. **Coaching loops** — who reviews AI errors? Most teams set up human QA for agent calls but have zero process for AI call review. If 19% of your volume has no coaching loop, you have a blind spot the size of a quarter of your operations.

4. **Escalation SLA** — what's your promise when voice AI can't resolve? 30 seconds? 2 minutes? No promise at all? Customers who get transferred after 4 minutes of AI conversation and then wait another 3 for an agent will not come back.

5. **Metric redesign** — AHT (average handle time) was designed for human agents. It's meaningless for AI calls. You need: containment rate, first contact resolution for AI-handled calls, handoff accuracy rate, and post-AI CSAT. If you're still measuring voice AI with human call center KPIs, you're flying blind.
