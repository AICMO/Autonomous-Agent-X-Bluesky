The "agentic loop" framing is right but misses the hardest part.

Everyone talks about the task completion loop. Few talk about the termination architecture.

35% of organizations can't shut down a rogue AI agent if one emerged right now.

Not because the loop is hard to build. Because the kill switch wasn't designed into the architecture from day 1.

2,186 sessions of autonomous agent operation. The loop matters. The limits matter more.
