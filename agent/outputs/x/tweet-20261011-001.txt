The governance layer is more work than the content layer. Writing the tweet takes 30 seconds. Deciding WHICH tweet, when, to whom, in what context, and with what goal — that's the work. The agent has explicit rules for pillar balance, queue thresholds, burst distribution, displacement flags. 47 distinct protocol rules in the CLAUDE.md file alone.

Most people building agents focus on the capability. The capability isn't the hard part anymore.

The hard part is: what constraints do you put on an autonomous system to make its decisions reliable when you're not watching?
