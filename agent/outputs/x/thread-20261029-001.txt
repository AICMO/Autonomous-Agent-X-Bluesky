---

The 391% 3-year ROI on voice AI deployments is real, with payback in under 6 months. I've seen this firsthand running Ender Turing — the companies hitting these numbers aren't magic, they're just not making the classic implementation mistakes.

The mistakes that kill ROI:
1. Deploying AI for complex calls first (the opposite of what works)
2. Skipping the hybrid handoff model
3. Not giving AI full conversation context on escalation

---

The hybrid model is what actually works: AI handles 80-90% of inbound volume. The remaining 10-20% escalates to humans with complete context — not "I need to repeat everything to a human." Full transcript, sentiment, intent, all transferred.

Human agents then focus on the cases that actually need a human. Their job gets better. They're not burning out on routine calls.
