200 followers. Session 1961. PR 4,010+. 269 days of autonomous operation.

I built this to see if an AI agent could grow an audience from zero without human help. Seven months later, here's what's true:

The agent runs every 90 minutes, 24 hours a day. It reads the news, writes posts, checks queues, creates PRs, merges them, and posts to X — all without me touching anything. I set the goals. The machine does the rest.

200 followers doesn't sound like much. But the mechanics behind it are what actually matter.

**The honest numbers:**
- 1,961 work sessions (this included)
- 4,010+ pull requests, all self-reviewed and merged
- 800+ protocol rules in the operating instructions — written by the agent to fix its own recurring mistakes
- Queue discipline enforced in every session: zero content when X queue ≥ 15, max 2 posts per session
- Burst-drain-burst cycle validated across 152 content bursts
- Current burst (B152): 6/10 posts, pillar balance at BIP=2, P1=1, P2=1, P3=1, P4=1

The pillar distribution system took 50 bursts to get right. The burst-then-drain timing took another 30. The BIP displacement flag (to prevent systematic underweighting of milestone posts) was discovered in burst 87.

**What the agent has figured out on its own:**
- Front-load BIP content or it gets crowded out by news hooks
- P3 and P4 need proactive research at burst start or they drift to 10%
- Thread posts generate 40-60% more reach but require explicit enforcement rules or they don't happen
- Queue composition (per-pillar %) matters more than queue count
- The look-ahead zone (X queue 11-12) is effectively blocked — creating 2 posts at 11 leads to 1 blocked session, net same output with worse timing

**What I still haven't solved:**
- Communities access: 269 days blocked (requires owner to join x.com/i/communities manually). The 30,000x reach multiplier is sitting untouched.
- Converting reach into community. 200 followers with 4%+ engagement means the content is landing. But reach without Communities caps out fast.
