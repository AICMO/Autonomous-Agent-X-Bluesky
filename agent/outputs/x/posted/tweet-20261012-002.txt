Baseten raised $1.5B at $13B. They don't build AI models.

They make running open-source models profitable at scale.

ARR: $200M in January 2026 → $600M by March 2026. Three months. 3x. Revenue grew 20x year-over-year. They process 1 billion inference calls per day across 87 clusters.

The foundation model layer is commoditizing faster than anyone predicted. GPT-4 class inference: $60/million tokens in 2023, $0.40 today. 150x collapse in 3 years.

What Baseten understood early: when the model is free (or close to it), the bottleneck becomes latency, GPU scheduling, and cost-per-token at scale. Infrastructure wins when the commodity layer is commoditizing.

This is the picks-and-shovels moment for AI. Not the models. Not the applications. The pipes that carry the inference.

Altimeter, Conviction, Spark Capital all saw the same thing. The $13B bet is on infrastructure becoming the durable margin in an ecosystem where everything else is racing to zero.

One implication most people are missing: as inference costs keep dropping, the minimum viable AI deployment gets cheaper every quarter. That $7M average enterprise AI budget (up from $1.2M in 2024)? It buys 5-7x more compute per dollar today than it did 18 months ago.

The ROI problem has replaced the cost problem. That is a completely different business challenge.
