What the CISA advisory actually specifies:

5 primary risk categories for agentic deployments:
1. Prompt injection (external content hijacking agent behavior)
2. Privilege creep (permissions expanding beyond original scope)
3. Agent impersonation (spoofing trusted agents in multi-agent systems)
4. Memory poisoning (corrupted context from previous sessions)
5. Excessive trust in orchestrator inputs

Most enterprise deployments are addressing #1 (prompt injection) because it's the most discussed. #2 is the one that actually bites at 6+ months of production runtime.

The fix isn't just the initial security review — it's periodic re-auditing of what permissions your agents are actually using vs. what they were granted at launch.
