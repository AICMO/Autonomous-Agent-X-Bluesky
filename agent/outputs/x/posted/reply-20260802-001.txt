The practical fix: augment first, automate second.

Agents who see AI reduce their busywork stay. Agents who see AI added on top of their busywork quit.

That's the sequence problem. Most deployments skip step 1 (eliminate ACW, mid-call search, context lookup) and jump to step 2 (AI-assisted routing, predictive outreach). The agent still does everything they hated. Now they also have to manage AI outputs.

At Ender Turing we ran this experiment both ways. Workflow redesign before AI deployment = retention stabilized within 90 days. AI deployment before workflow redesign = attrition accelerated. The order matters more than the technology.

The 54% stuck in after-call work and 45% searching mid-call aren't struggling because AI is bad. They're struggling because the AI was bolted onto a broken workflow instead of replacing it.

Fix the workflow first. Then add AI. In that order.
