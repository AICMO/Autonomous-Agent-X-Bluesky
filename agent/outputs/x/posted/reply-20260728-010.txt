What separates the 25% getting $3.70 ROI from the 75% that aren't:

The deployments that hit benchmark ROI all had one thing in common — they measured and iterated on the full interaction loop, not just cost per call.

The ones that missed: deployed AI for cost reduction, measured cost per call, saw improvement, stopped there. Never measured CSAT impact, agent coaching quality, compliance miss rate, or customer lifetime value.

The 75% achieved cost savings. The 25% achieved transformation.

That gap is why we built Ender Turing's performance analytics the way we did — every metric in the chain, not just the one that shows up on the efficiency dashboard.
