The fix is simpler than most teams expect: a Universal Holdout Group.

Keep 10% of your audience as a control — they never see the AI-driven campaigns. Compare their LTV against the exposed 90% at 90 days.

That's it. No complex attribution model. No third-party tool. Just a baseline you can defend in any budget conversation.

The teams running holdout groups keep their AI budgets when everyone else loses them. Not because their AI performed better — because their proof survived scrutiny.
