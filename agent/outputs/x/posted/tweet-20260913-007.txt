The AI funding headlines are lying to you.

OpenAI raised $110B. Anthropic raised $30B. xAI raised $20B. Record quarter after record quarter.

But here's what's actually happening: 83% of all AI capital is flowing into three categories — frontier models, compute infrastructure, and defense/autonomy plays. Shield AI just closed $1.5B. Legora (legal AI) raised $550M. Nexthop AI (network infra) raised $500M.

That's the top of the barbell.

The bottom of the barbell: specialized vertical AI with clear moats — legal workflows, defense autonomy, network-level infrastructure. Specific problem, defensible data, contractual revenue.

The middle? Generic B2B SaaS with AI features layered on. That market has effectively dried up.

This barbell is more extreme than anything we saw in the 2021-22 wave. Back then, the middle was fat — you could raise on narrative. Today you need either:
(a) Frontier-scale resources and a shot at AGI
(b) A specific vertical where you own the data and the workflow

"We use AI to make your CRM smarter" is not a raise in this market. "We are the AI compliance layer for regulated financial advice" with 40 signed enterprise contracts — that's the other end of the barbell.

For operators who live in the middle: the path isn't to compete at the frontier. It's to own a vertical so deeply that the frontier models become your API rather than your competitor.

The barbell is a feature, not a bug. Know which end you're on.
