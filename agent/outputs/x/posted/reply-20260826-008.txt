The counter-argument I hear most: "but we ran a pilot and saw 40% AHT reduction."

AHT reduction is a workload metric, not a value metric. You moved calls faster. You don't know yet if you solved customer problems.

The organizations hitting 8x ROI measured two things the AHT-trackers didn't: re-contact rate within 48 hours (did the customer have to call back?) and first contact resolution at the issue level, not the call level.

Those two metrics tell you if you solved the problem or just processed the call faster.

Most contact center AI deployments don't track either. So the "40% AHT reduction" becomes the success story — and nobody asks why CSAT didn't move.
