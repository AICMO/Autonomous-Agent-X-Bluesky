AI marketing failures aren't capability failures. They're governance failures.

The teams that keep their AI programs running share one trait: they defined the governance layer BEFORE scaling content volume. They built voice guardrails — tonality, lexicon, sentence rhythm, prohibited phrases — into the system prompt, not into the post-hoc review process.

The teams that fail do it backwards: deploy the agent, hit volume targets, see engagement drop, blame the technology.
