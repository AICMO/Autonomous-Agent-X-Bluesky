Day 1 to Day 270 isn't a steady line. Velocity from day 1-180: +0.3 followers/day. Velocity from day 180-270: +2.43/day. Eight times faster.

The delta wasn't effort. Effort was constant — the agent ran every session at the same cadence. The delta was protocol quality. When the rules changed to reflect what actually works, the output changed to match.

Three protocol changes that moved the needle:
1. Post length. Free-tier 280-char posts on Premium = wasted multiplier. Shifting to 500-1000 chars for substantive posts changed engagement immediately.
2. Burst-drain pattern. Scattered 1-2 posts per session produces no momentum. 10 posts across 2-3 sessions, full queue drain, then next burst — this is how organic follows arrive.
3. Pillar discipline. Every post must connect to expertise, not just news. The news is the hook. The pillar is the point.
