Annual attrition for most contact centers is already 30-45%. That's not a talent problem. That's a structural problem.

And the thing about contact center attrition is that it's expensive in a very specific way. Not just recruiting costs. The real cost is the experience gap between a new agent (month 1-6) and a seasoned agent (year 2-3). New agents take longer, make more errors, escalate more calls. Your customers feel the difference. Your CSAT scores reflect it.

This is exactly the window where voice AI makes the most economic sense — not to replace agents, but to close the experience gap while you're perpetually rebuilding your workforce.

Voice AI in 2026: ~$0.40 per call vs $7-12 for a human agent. That's not a rounding error. That's a 90-95% cost reduction per contact on the interactions AI handles.

Companies that deployed voice AI early are seeing 26.7% lift in revenue and 32.6% gain in CSAT scores. The AI handles routine volume. Human agents get the complex calls they were actually hired for. Attrition drops because agents aren't burned out on repetitive work.
