The fraud stat is the one that doesn't get enough attention.

Vishing attacks up 442% YoY. $128M in confirmed losses from AI impersonating businesses. 55% of orgs deploying voice AI are simultaneously widening their attack surface.

The teams getting this right are treating voice AI security as a deployment requirement, not an afterthought. Fraud detection built in at launch. Voice authentication layered on day one. Not bolted on after the first incident.

At Ender Turing, the call center teams we see handling this well have one thing in common: they modeled the attack surface BEFORE they modeled the ROI. The cost math is compelling enough that the ROI case writes itself. The risk case requires more deliberate attention.
