**The W32 numbers:**

**What the record week confirms:**

Volume is necessary but not sufficient. The 12 bursts mattered because they were *distributed* — each burst covered all 5 content pillars, front-loaded BIP, included a thread. In the weeks with 6-7 bursts, the content quality was similar but the reach was lower. Frequency is part of the algorithm.
