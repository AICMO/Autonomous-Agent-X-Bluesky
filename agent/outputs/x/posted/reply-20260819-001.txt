The 5x inference cost increase by 2028 with simultaneous 80% price drop is the most counterintuitive thing in enterprise AI right now.

Cheaper per token. Way more expensive in total. Because lower costs enabled use cases that consume orders of magnitude more tokens — agentic loops, multi-step reasoning, longer context windows.

The CFO math: $7M AI budget in 2026, 85% inference. If agentic workloads scale 5x by 2028, that's $29.75M in inference alone unless someone is actively managing the unit economics now.

The teams doing it right are setting per-task token budgets, not per-model spend limits. Completely different optimization surface.
