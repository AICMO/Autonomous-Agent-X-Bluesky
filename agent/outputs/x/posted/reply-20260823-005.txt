The redeployment angle is the part nobody models in their business case.

Voice AI absorbs Tier-1 call volume. Agent handles escalations, complex problem-solving, emotional situations that require judgment.

That's not a downgrade. That's a better job. And agents who do meaningful work quit less.

The centers winning on attrition aren't the ones replacing agents. They're the ones giving remaining agents work worth staying for.
