171% ROI on agentic AI. 75% of outputs still need human editing. Both numbers are correct.

The organizations expecting to eliminate human judgment from content operations are hitting the wall. The ones scaling agentic AI successfully learned a different lesson: the value isn't AI replaces humans. It's AI makes each human hour produce more.

Here's where the gap shows up:
- 25% of AI-generated content requires editing for brand voice alignment
- 31% needs factual accuracy correction
- 19% requires strategic messaging adjustments

That's 75% of outputs touched by a human before they go out. And the ROI is still 171%.

The math holds because you're not measuring "AI writes, human doesn't." You're measuring time-per-unit-of-output. A team that previously produced 40 content pieces per month now produces 200. Even with 75% requiring edits, the per-unit human time dropped 60-70%.

The teams failing are deploying for replacement. The teams winning are deploying for throughput.

Marketing automation ROI isn't about labor elimination. It's about leverage — each human decision applied to dramatically more output. When you set up measurement frameworks for throughput (pieces per person-hour) instead of automation rate (% requiring no edits), the 171% becomes understandable instead of surprising.

The 75% editing requirement isn't a failure. It's the honest metric.
