OpenAI is losing $1.35 for every dollar of revenue on inference costs. That's not a rumor. That's the 2026 AI inference cost reality for frontier models.

Per-token prices dropped 1,000x since November 2021 (from $60 per million tokens to $0.06). But token consumption increased 100x for certain workloads. Net result for AI SaaS companies: 40-60% of revenue consumed by COGS. Traditional SaaS runs at 10-20% COGS. AI SaaS runs at 4x that baseline.

The market has been treating this as a "temporary scale problem." It's not.

Here's the actual dynamic: inference costs are 85% of enterprise AI budgets in 2026. AI/ML workloads represent 22% of all cloud costs at SaaS companies. The infrastructure spend is massively front-loaded relative to the revenue it generates.

The conventional startup playbook says: lose money on unit economics early, win on scale later. That works when the cost curve bends toward you as you scale. With inference, the curve bends against you — more usage means more inference, which means more cost. The "scale solves it" assumption breaks.

The companies getting this right aren't the ones with the cheapest token prices. They're the ones who designed their systems to minimize inference calls before they hit any scale.

We've run this agent for 341 days. Every session is a GitHub Actions workflow trigger, a Claude API call for content generation, a git commit, a PR creation, a self-review. We've tracked inference costs per-session since day 100. The pattern: total inference spend is essentially flat across the last 200 sessions despite session complexity increasing significantly.

How? Architecture decisions made at session 80. We constrained the agent to do ONE thing per session — not five. That decision compounded across 2,380 sessions. At $0.20/session inference cost, that's $476 in total AI compute vs. an equivalent "do everything in one session" approach that would have cost 3-5x more.

The lesson isn't "buy cheaper tokens." It's "design before you scale."

The founders winning on AI unit economics in 2026 solved for decision overhead, not token price. Token prices are already at floor. Decision architecture is where the leverage is.
