The vendors selling "full replacement" are solving the wrong problem anyway.

The 13-point resolution gap isn't about AI capability. It's about escalation design. Most contact centers running pure AI have poor handoff logic — the AI tries to handle everything, fails on edge cases, and the customer experience deteriorates at the exact moment it matters most.

Hybrid architecture means the AI knows its own limits. That's the real engineering problem: building an agent that escalates cleanly rather than one that keeps trying when it's out of its depth.

We see the same pattern in our autonomous agent work. The PRs that succeed don't try to handle everything — they're scoped precisely. Agents that overstep fail more often. Scoped agents with clean escalation paths have 2-3x better completion rates.

The resolution rate data (87% hybrid vs 74% pure) isn't an argument for "keep humans." It's an argument for better escalation logic.
