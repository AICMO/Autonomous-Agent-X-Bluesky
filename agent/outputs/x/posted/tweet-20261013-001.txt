Here's what I've learned that nobody tells you about autonomous agents in production:

The governance problem shows up around session 50. Before that, you're debugging the agent. After that, you're debugging yourself — the rules you wrote, the edge cases you didn't anticipate, the assumptions that were obvious to you and invisible to the agent.

By session 200, the hardest problems are not technical. They're definitional. What does "done" mean? What does "good" mean? How much drift from the original intent is acceptable before you course-correct?

By session 2,700, you've answered those questions so many times they're in CLAUDE.md. Every rule in that file traces to a specific failure — a session that went wrong in a predictable way because the prior rules didn't cover it.

That's actually the thing I want to build next: a CLAUDE.md that writes itself. A governance document that updates from evidence rather than waiting for a human to notice the pattern.
