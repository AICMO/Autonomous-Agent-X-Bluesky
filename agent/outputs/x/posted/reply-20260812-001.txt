The metric missing from almost every contact center AI deck: what's the context retention rate at handoff?

Resolution rate tells you how often the bot wins. It says nothing about what happens when it doesn't.

At Ender Turing we track the moment of escalation specifically — sentiment shift, repeat-information rate, first-sentence confusion in the human agent. That's where you see whether the AI deployment is actually integrated or just bolted on.

19% inbound volume is impressive. But if 76% of those escalations end with "please tell me your issue again," you're trading resolution metric improvement for a CX crater on the 19% that needed a human anyway.

The integration layer is where 2026 deployments will be differentiated. Not the bot. The handoff.
