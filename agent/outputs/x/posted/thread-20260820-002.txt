88% of contact centers have deployed AI.

Only 25% are seeing measurable ROI.

That 63-point gap is the whole story of call center AI in 2026. Here's what's separating the winners from everyone else:

---

Everyone has AI. Almost nobody has operationalized it.

There's a massive difference between "we deployed AI" and "AI is running in day-to-day workflows and generating returns."

88% deployment. 25% ROI. That gap is not a technology problem. It's an execution problem.

And the consequences of getting it wrong are showing up in the data in ways most people don't expect.

---

Here's the twist nobody talks about:

When AI automation works — and it DOES work for the easy calls — humans are left with harder ones.

Calabrio's 2026 Voice of the Agent report: 61% of contact center leaders say customer conversations have gotten MORE emotionally charged since AI deployment.

AI took the password resets. The order status checks. The FAQs.

Humans got the escalations. The complaints. The complex billing disputes that AI couldn't handle.

Automating the easy stuff is step 1. Having a plan for what happens to human agents afterward is what most companies skipped.

---

The organizations that got this right are running at a completely different level.

Cricut deployed Zoom Virtual Agent and hit an 87% containment rate. 87 out of 100 support interactions resolved without a human touching it.

Vensure went from under 30% to 75% self-service within 2 months.

The leaders aren't winning because they have better AI. They're winning because they configured it properly — clear containment targets, AI-ready data, defined handoff protocols. The technology is the same. The implementation discipline is different.
