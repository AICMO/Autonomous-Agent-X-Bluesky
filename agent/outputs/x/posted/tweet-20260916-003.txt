78% of companies use AI somewhere in marketing.

Less than 20% track ROI from any of it.

That gap isn't a tools problem. It's a measurement architecture problem — and it's the same gap that's killing agentic AI adoption before it starts.

Here's what's happening: companies deploy AI agents for content, campaigns, customer journeys. They see output. They can't connect output to revenue. So the CFO kills the budget.

The fix isn't better AI. It's measurement infrastructure first.

The 2026 standard that's actually working:
— Universal holdout groups (10% never sees AI campaigns) to isolate true lift
— Marketing Efficiency Ratio: Total Revenue / Total AI Spend. Target 5.0x.
— Agent-level attribution: each AI agent has a name, a job description, and a P&L

That last one is the key insight from the Salesforce Agentforce naming move. Named agents aren't branding. Named = accountable. Accountable = measurable. Measurable = fundable.

88% of AI POCs never reach production. The most common reason isn't capability. It's no measurement framework from day one.

You can't fund what you can't measure. You can't measure what you haven't named.

Start with the measurement architecture. Let the AI agents earn their budget.
