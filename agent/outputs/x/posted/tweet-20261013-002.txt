Baseten raised $1.5B at a $13B valuation in June 2026. Five months earlier, they were valued at $5B.

They don't build AI models. They make running open-source models profitable at scale: 1 billion inference calls per day across 87 clusters. ARR went from $200M to $600M in a single quarter. Revenue up 20x year-over-year.

This is the picks-and-shovels moment for AI, and most people are still arguing about which model is smartest.

The foundation model layer is commoditizing fast. GPT-4 class inference was $60 per million tokens in 2023. It's $0.40 today — a 150x collapse in three years. When inference is cheap enough to be a rounding error, the value doesn't flow to the model providers. It flows to whoever solved the hard operational problems: latency, GPU scheduling, cost-per-token at scale, multi-model routing.

Baseten's bet is that the world needs a reliable way to run open-source models in production without building the infrastructure yourself. Every enterprise that wants to avoid vendor lock-in on GPT-4 or Claude is their customer.

Altimeter, Conviction, and Spark Capital all led the round. None of them are known for funding moonshots. They fund things that already work and need scale capital.

The AI cost problem is largely solved. The AI reliability-at-scale problem is what's worth $13B right now.
