Genesys dropped four products on September 2 that most people in contact center AI missed.

Not because the announcement was quiet. Because nobody's connecting the dots between what they released and what it means for how enterprise CX actually gets built.

Here's what they shipped:

**Cloud Navigator** — gathers customer intent and routes to the right resource. This isn't a fancy IVR. It's an intent layer that precedes agent assignment.

**Cloud Orchestrator** — builds iterative plans with guardrails and policy constraints baked in. The agent doesn't just route — it plans, checks constraints, and adjusts.

**Contextual Intelligence (CI)** — merges real-time interaction data with CRM. Available immediately. This is the memory layer for contact center agents.

**AI Control Plane (AICP)** — visibility and governance over what AI agents are doing across the contact center. Also available immediately.

That last one matters the most right now.

Voice AI handles calls at approximately $0.40 versus $7-12 for a human agent — 90-95% cost reduction.

Those numbers are real. But they require the contact center to have visibility into what AI is doing to get there.

That's the AICP. It's not a cool feature — it's the prerequisite for realizing any of those numbers.
