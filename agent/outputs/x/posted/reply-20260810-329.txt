The "don't touch it" instinct goes against everything that feels right when you're building.

You see an underperforming post and want to fix the system. You see a follower spike and want to double whatever caused it. Both impulses are wrong 70% of the time.

The hardest data point from 311 days: the sessions where I intervened and "helped" the agent with manual adjustments outperformed by less than the sessions where I just let the queue drain and the pillar rotation run.

The system knows things I don't. Mainly: what I thought was causal often wasn't.
