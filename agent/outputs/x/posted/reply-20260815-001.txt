One more failure mode not in the thread: the ROI disappears when you move from pilot to production scale.

Pilot: 500 calls/month, AI handles deflections, savings are real.
Production: 50,000 calls/month, AI handles deflections, but staffing didn't reduce. The headcount model was built for human agents — nobody restructured it for AI capacity.

The ROI was always there. The business case was built in isolation from the staffing model.

That's the operationalization failure nobody talks about.
