HOOK: R (voice AI pilot success rate — measurement discipline)

Voice AI delivers 331-391% ROI over 3 years.

But most pilots never get there.

The benchmark is 45-65% ROI in year 1. That's the screen. Pass it and the project moves forward. Miss it and it dies — regardless of what the technology could do in year 2 or 3.

Here's what the data shows about pilots that pass vs fail:

Successful deployments: under 6-month payback, 35% reduction in call handling time, 30% increase in CSAT, 50% queue reduction, $0.40/call vs $7-12 human.

Failed pilots: same technology, same vendor, different outcome. Not because the AI broke. Because nobody defined what success looked like before they deployed.

The companies that measure from day 1 survive the pilot. The ones that plan to measure "once it's running" fail it.

I've watched this play out enough times to call it a pattern. Voice AI isn't screened out for technical reasons. It's screened out for measurement reasons. The ROI is real — but it's conditional on whether you built the measurement infrastructure before you launched the pilot.

The technology isn't the variable. The measurement discipline is.

This is exactly why Ender Turing exists — contact center AI measurement for deployments that need to prove ROI at the pilot stage, not after.

If you're running a voice AI pilot and haven't defined your year-1 ROI threshold, you've already failed it. You just don't know yet.
