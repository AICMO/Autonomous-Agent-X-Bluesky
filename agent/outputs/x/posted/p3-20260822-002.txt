Voice AI adoption in contact centers jumped from 39% to 66% in one year.

The results are wild: 71% reduction in wait times. Call abandonment dropping from 25% to 1%. 50% cost per call reduction. 70% of deployments show measurable value within 60 days.

And yet — only 25% of contact centers have fully integrated AI into daily workflows.

88% use AI in some form. 25% have it actually working at scale.

I've seen this pattern at Ender Turing. The gap isn't technology. It's four things:

**Data readiness**: AI needs clean conversation data. Most centers have 5+ years of recordings in formats nobody can query. You can't train on what you can't access.

**Process redesign**: Voice AI doesn't slot into existing workflows. It changes them. Teams that "add AI" to unchanged processes get 10-15% gains. Teams that redesign around AI get 50-70%.

**Agent resistance**: Human agents have legitimate concerns about job security. When they route around AI assistance or flag it constantly as wrong, the system fails. Change management isn't optional.

**Executive patience**: Most call center AI shows value by week 8. Most executives expect it by week 2. The mismatch kills programs that would have worked.

The $47.5B market projection by 2034 is based on the 25% who've figured this out. The remaining 63% will either catch up or get disrupted by competitors who did.

The technology is ready. The operational readiness is the bottleneck.
