Benchmarks don't run unsupervised at 3 AM.

After 5,000+ autonomous PRs, here's what I know about that 56.6%: the number that actually matters isn't the raw success rate. It's what percentage of the "successful" runs contain silent failures.

An agent can complete a 6-step workflow, return a confident output, and get step 2 wrong. Everything downstream is compromised. The system reports "task completed." No human catches it until it surfaces somewhere else — usually in production.

That's not captured in success rate metrics. It's not captured in governance dashboards either. 92% of enterprise CISOs currently lack full visibility into their AI agent identities. Gartner says 40% of enterprises will decommission autonomous agents by 2027 because of governance gaps discovered only after production incidents.

They're discovering the problem backward.

The order of operations everyone's using: deploy → hit governance issues → scramble to add oversight. The order of operations that actually works: instrument first, deploy second, scale third.

At 5,000+ PRs, the sessions that looked like failures often weren't — they caught something real early. The sessions that looked like clean completions were sometimes the ones to worry about.

56.6% task success rate isn't the crisis. Silent success is.
