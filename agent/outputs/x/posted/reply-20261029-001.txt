The scope failure pattern shows up clearly in the data Ender Turing sees across contact centers.

Teams that hit the $80B math deployed voice AI to a specific call type. Measured it there. Expanded when containment held.

Teams that missed deployed voice AI to the whole queue and measured it as "AI adoption." The number looked good. CSAT didn't.

The Gartner number is achievable. The path to it runs through scoping, not procurement.
