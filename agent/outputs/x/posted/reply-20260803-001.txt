The attrition math makes this worse than it looks.

If 54% of calls require ACW and 45% require mid-call search — those aren't just time costs. They're the exact tasks that make agents feel like data entry clerks. The repetitive lookup-gather-document loop on every call.

The centers seeing the biggest attrition reduction aren't the ones with the most automation. They're the ones that automated specifically the tasks agents told them were most soul-crushing. That sequence matters: survey agents first, then automate.

It's a different project than "deploy AI to reduce AHT." Same technology, different frame.
