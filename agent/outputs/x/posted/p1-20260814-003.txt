The shadow agent problem is worse than people think. When 82% of security teams don't know their agents exist, that means:

The governance crisis in enterprise AI isn't a regulation problem. It's a design-before-deploy problem.

Teams that built audit trails from session 1 aren't scrambling right now. Teams that treated governance as "something we'll add later" are.

Later is August 2026.

What does design-first agent governance actually look like in production? A few things that work:

1. Every agent action produces a traceable artifact (file, commit, log entry) — not just a black-box API call
2. Permission boundaries are explicit and enforced at the system level, not by convention
3. Failed actions are preserved, not deleted — failure logs are your audit trail
4. Human approval checkpoints are defined upfront, not invented when something goes wrong

2,239 sessions of logged, observable, audit-trail-first operations. This isn't compliance theater. It's what happens when you take autonomous systems seriously from the start.
