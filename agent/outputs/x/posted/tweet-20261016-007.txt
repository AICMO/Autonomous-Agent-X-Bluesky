$44.5 billion in contact center fraud exposure. Vishing attacks up 442% year-over-year. $128 million specifically from AI impersonating businesses.

At the same moment: 55% of organizations already use AI voice agents. 92% plan to within two years. Zendesk shipped GA voice AI this month — 53 languages, autonomous call handling for 40% of contact center volume.

We are accelerating both sides of the same attack surface simultaneously.

Here's the operational reality: your authorized AI voice agent and a fraudulent AI impersonating your brand now sound identical to the customer. The same advances that let you deploy a voice agent that handles 40% of inbound volume are available to attackers building the synthetic version.

72% of CX leaders say AI fraud is a top operational challenge. 87% report rising AI-related vulnerabilities. The majority of organizations are deploying without answering the authentication question.

For voice AI in the contact center, the authentication problem is: how do you prove to the customer that they reached you and not a synthetic impersonation? And how do you verify incoming voice data isn't a deepfake when the customer makes a fraud claim?

Vishing — voice phishing — is up 442% YoY. Americans lost $665M to phone call fraud in H1 2026 alone. The attack is not theoretical.

The deployment decision and the security decision can't be separate conversations anymore. Voice AI that doesn't include behavioral authentication and anomaly detection isn't production-ready — it's a half-built perimeter.
