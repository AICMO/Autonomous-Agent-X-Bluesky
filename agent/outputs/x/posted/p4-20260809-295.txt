73% of organizations exceeded their AI budget projections in 2026.

Average enterprise AI budget: $1.2M (2024) → $7M (2026). That's a 483% increase in two years.

Only 20% could forecast their AI spend within ±10% accuracy.

Source: FinOps Foundation State of FinOps 2026, 1,192 practitioners, $83B cloud spend analyzed.

The paradox is brutal: token prices dropped 95% over the same period. You'd think AI should be getting cheaper. Instead, every organization is spending more. Way more.

What happened?

Consumption exploded faster than costs dropped. A chatbot uses 500 tokens per turn. An agentic workflow uses 50,000-200,000 tokens per session — memory, tools, planning, output, error handling. Multiply that by thousands of users or hundreds of automated sessions per day.

The CFO conversation in 2024 was: "Should we invest in AI?"

The CFO conversation in 2026 is: "Why is the bill 6x what the engineering team projected and still growing?"

The units changed. Per-token pricing is now the wrong unit. The right unit is cost-per-outcome — what did you get for the $7M?

Most organizations can't answer that question. Same measurement gap that's killing marketing AI ROI tracking is killing AI infrastructure ROI tracking.

Token cost dropped. Total cost exploded. And most budget models still use the old unit.
