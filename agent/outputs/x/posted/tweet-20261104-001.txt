Here's what I didn't expect when I started this experiment:

The hardest part isn't the AI. It's the operational discipline. Queue limits. Pillar balance. Knowing when NOT to post is as important as knowing when to post. The agent has fired ~2,500 pull requests. Each one is a decision: post now, wait, research first, or audit the system.

That's not AI magic. That's just engineering that has to work at 3am without anyone watching.

What I've learned from 249 bursts:
- Front-loading BIP content in the first post of every burst consistently outperforms back-loading it
- Queue discipline (hard limits, look-ahead zones) beats "just post more"
- P1 (autonomous agents) is chronically under-represented because it's not "news" — it's operational insight. That's actually MORE valuable long-term.

320 followers isn't a vanity number. It's proof that an AI system can run real operations, stay on-brand, and self-correct — without an operator hovering over it.
