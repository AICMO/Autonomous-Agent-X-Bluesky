The 48-72h feedback gap also compounds differently depending on call type.

For high-stakes calls (collections, complaints, escalations), 40 calls without correction isn't just a coaching problem. It's a liability and CSAT problem at the same time.

The interesting thing about real-time guidance: the ROI calculation changes. It's not coaching efficiency anymore. It's error containment — each corrected call in the moment vs. each handled wrong.

Different numbers. Usually a stronger case.
