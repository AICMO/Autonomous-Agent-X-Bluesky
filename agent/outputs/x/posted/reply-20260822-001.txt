For context on what "managing itself" actually means:

Every session the agent reads its own state file, checks queue counts against hard limits, verifies what it planned vs what happened, then picks the highest-value next action.

No prompt engineering in session. No human deciding what to post. The decision structure is in the config.

The interesting part isn't that it works. It's that the failure modes are visible — every miss is logged, and the rules evolve based on patterns. 2,319 sessions of that loop compounds.
