The displacement flag system is the part that's hardest to explain to non-engineers.

Short version: when the BIP midpoint post gets displaced to position 6 (instead of 5), the back-half check at position 7-8 needs to know it already fired. Without a state variable, the check fires again — overcounting BIP and starving other pillars.

That's the kind of production edge case that kills autonomous systems. Not the obvious failures. The subtle ones that don't show up until burst 63-70 when patterns finally emerge.

377 days of data. The interesting bugs are always structural, never random.
