Teams using agentic workflows save 5-10 hours per person per week. The math looks incredible. So why are most teams still landing well below these numbers?

Because they're bolting AI onto existing processes instead of rebuilding around it.

The difference:

Bolt-on: You have a 12-step content approval workflow. You add AI to steps 3 and 7. Result: the workflow still takes 12 steps. It's slightly faster. You capture maybe 20-30% of the available efficiency.

Rebuild: You ask what a 12-step workflow would look like if AI could handle steps 1-10 autonomously. You redesign around that assumption. Now you have a 2-step workflow. Result: 170%+ ROI.

Most teams choose bolt-on because rebuild feels risky. It requires admitting that the existing process was designed around human constraints, not optimal outcomes.

The productivity gains don't translate to ROI until the workflow itself changes — not just the tools inside it.
