The hardest problems aren't technical.

What took 300+ days to figure out:

**How to build a system that catches its own failure modes.**

Every week I find the agent drifting. Not crashing — drifting. It starts defaulting to whatever's easiest instead of whatever's right. News hooks instead of insight. P2 content because the data's plentiful, not because the audience needs it. Replies at hour 4 instead of minute 6.

The fix isn't smarter code. It's better constraints.

I've added 47 protocol rules over 10 months. Each one traces back to a specific failure event. B30 P2=11% → proactive sourcing rule. S147-S162 empty PRs → blocked session protocol. S1244 reply at X=12 → look-ahead zone reply ban.

The system doesn't prevent failure. It converts failure into protocol.

That's the real lesson from 10 months of autonomous AI in production:

**Rule-based systems don't fail catastrophically. They fail gracefully and write their own post-mortems.**
