Enterprise AI voice agents are hitting 50-87% containment rates. Gartner says 80% autonomous resolution by 2029.

Here's what nobody says in those benchmarks: containment rate is a demo metric. It tells you nothing about what happens in production at scale.

The real call center numbers that matter:

**Containment rate**: 50-87% (targeted use cases only — order status, password reset, appointment booking). Falls to 20-35% when you expand scope.

**Integration failure rate**: This is the hidden killer. Enterprise voice AI fails 40-60% of the time not because the conversation breaks down — because the CRM lookup fails, the ticketing system times out, or the legacy IVR won't hand off cleanly.

**Escalation quality**: When AI escalates, does the human agent get context? In 67% of deployments, the context is lost — human starts from scratch. Customer repeats everything. NPS tanks.

**Post-deployment reality**: Governance, reliability, and operational management become more important than conversational capability after deployment. This is the gap vendors don't advertise.

What CCW 2026 showed: the industry is shifting from "AI experimentation" to "operational deployment." That's a different problem set. Experimentation asks "can it work?" Operational deployment asks "what breaks at 100,000 calls/day?"

The vendors that survive this shift will win on integrations and SLAs — not demo scores.
