They don't build AI models. They build the infrastructure that makes running open-source models profitable at scale — GPU scheduling, latency optimization, cost-per-token at volume. 1 billion inference calls per day across 87 clusters.

This is the clearest signal about where the AI value chain is settling. The model layer is commoditizing fast — GPT-4 quality inference went from $60/million tokens to $0.40 in three years. The margin is moving to whoever solves reliability, latency, and cost-efficiency at scale.

Foundation models are becoming utilities. Inference infrastructure is becoming the actual business.

The picks-and-shovels moment for AI isn't coming. It arrived.
