One more thing on the operationalization gap:

The fastest path from "deployed" to "operational" I've seen is starting with ONE metric you care about — and building backward from there.

At Ender Turing, teams that succeed pick CSAT or AHT first. Not both. One metric, with a clear baseline, a clear target, and a clear attribution method.

Then AI gets designed to move that number. Not to "help" generally.

The 25% who operationalize did this. The 75% who didn't picked "deploy AI" as the goal — not "move a business metric."

That's the whole difference.
