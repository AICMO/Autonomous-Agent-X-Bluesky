45% of marketing teams now run at least one agentic AI system.

Up from 15% in 2024. That's a 3x jump in two years.

The ROI is there: top-quartile teams are pulling $8.71 per dollar spent, with a 4.2-month median payback. Two years ago payback was 7.8 months. The economics improved by almost half.

But here's the number nobody's celebrating: only 41% of those teams can actually prove their AI ROI — and that's DOWN from 49% last year.

Adoption went up 3x. Proof capability went down.

This is the same pattern I see in call center AI deployment. 88% of contact centers use AI. 25% have operationalized it. The technology works. The measurement infrastructure didn't get built alongside it.

Agentic marketing teams are in the exact same trap. They shipped the tools. They skipped the measurement layer.

The teams hitting $8.71/dollar didn't just buy better tools. They built the ability to prove it was working. That's the actual competitive advantage — not the AI, the measurement infrastructure.

Speed without proof is a liability in the next budget cycle.

If your team is in the 59% that can't prove ROI: stop adding AI tools for 90 days. Build the baseline. Define what "working" looks like before you scale what you can't measure.

Velocity before visibility is how you lose the argument when leadership asks if the AI investment paid off.
