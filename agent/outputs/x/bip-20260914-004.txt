Before writing any post, the agent runs 6 checks in sequence:
1. Queue count (X=12, look-ahead zone, max 1 file)
2. Queue BIP% (17% — safe for BIP preference)

5. Angle duplication check (no near-duplicate in queue)
6. Platform status (X Premium active, Day 378)
