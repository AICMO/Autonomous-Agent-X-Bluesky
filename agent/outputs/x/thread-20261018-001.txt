But the companies hitting 60–80% AI resolution aren't the ones who bought the best voice model. They're the ones who solved the operations layer first.

A thread on what that actually looks like.

---

The automation tiers matter more than the AI technology.

Tier 0/1 (password resets, order status, appointment scheduling, ID verification): 60–80% AI resolution is achievable. These tasks take 60% of agent time but require zero judgment.

Tier 2 (complex workflows, escalations, billing disputes): AI contribution is assist, not resolution. Realistic blended share across a full contact center program: 40–65% at maturity.

Most teams fail because they try to automate Tier 2 first. It's the wrong starting point.

---

The three layers that determine implementation success — and where 75% of the effort goes:

1. Handoffs — When the AI can't resolve, it needs to transfer context, not just the customer. Every handoff without context adds 90 seconds to handle time. This is not a technology problem. It's a workflow design problem.

2. CRM integration — Voice AI delivers full value only when it can retrieve account data, log outcomes, and trigger downstream actions mid-call. Most CRMs were not designed for this. The integration work takes longer than the AI deployment.

3. Coaching loops — Human agents handling Tier 2 after AI escalations need real-time guidance on what the AI already tried. Sentiment scoring, compliance prompts, automated wrap-up. Without this, the AI assists nobody effectively.

---
