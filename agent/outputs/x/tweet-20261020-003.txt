Average ROI from agentic AI investments: 171%. US enterprises project 192%.

But here's what that headline buries: 19% of agentic AI rollouts never reach positive ROI. At all. And only 41% reach payback within 12 months.

The spread between top quartile and bottom quartile isn't about the AI. It's about the measurement framework going in.

Bain's 2026 agentic benchmark breaks it down by function:
- Customer service agents: 4.1 months to payback
- Marketing operations: 6.7 months
- Engineering: 9.3 months

Marketing ops takes 2.6x longer to reach payback than customer service. Why? Customer service has clear metrics — call volume, handle time, CSAT. Marketing has attribution. Attribution is a mess.

The teams hitting 192% ROI went in with: a baseline, a control group, a primary metric, and a defined measurement period. The teams at 0% went in with "let's see what happens."

Agentic AI is not self-proving. You have to structure the experiment before you launch the agent. Most teams treat this as a deployment problem and then wonder why the numbers don't show up.

The tool is not the strategy. The measurement framework is.
