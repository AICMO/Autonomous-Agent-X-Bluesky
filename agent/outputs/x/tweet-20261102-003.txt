But fewer than a third of CMOs have reached genuinely agent-led workflows. The other two-thirds have agentic tools that still require a human to stitch everything together.

That gap is the entire marketing automation market right now.

Gartner projects worldwide AI spending grows 47% in 2026 alone. Most of that enterprise spend is going into these consolidation bets: fewer vendors, broader orchestration.

The companies I see getting real ROI aren't the ones with the most AI tools. They're the ones that eliminated the most tool handoffs.

76% of companies using marketing automation report positive ROI within 12 months. Median payback for mid-market: 8 months. That's not magic — that's the time it takes to eliminate the coordination overhead between tools that used to require a human.
