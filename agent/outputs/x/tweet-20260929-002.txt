91% of companies claim they "use AI" for marketing.

Fewer than a third use it for anything that actually moves the needle.

The other 60%+ are paying AI vendors to write email subject lines faster.

This is the "digital transformation" problem all over again — but compressed into 18 months instead of 5 years.

Here's what the 33% who actually run agentic marketing workflows are doing that the others aren't.

They're automating decisions, not tasks.

Email subject line generation automates a task. A copywriter still makes all the decisions — what to write about, who to target, what offer to test, when to send. The AI just types faster.

Agentic marketing automation replaces decision loops. The system decides what content to produce based on performance data, who gets which variant based on behavioral signals, when to escalate a lead to a human, which channels to prioritize this week.

The ROI difference is massive. Agentic deployments that replace full decision-making workflows deliver 544% ROI over 3 years. Legacy task automation: 195%. Same budget. Very different game.

The tell: ask a marketing team "what decisions does your AI make?" Not "what tasks does it do?" — decisions.

If the answer is "it helps us write content faster," they're in the 67%.

If the answer includes "it decides what to test, who to target, and when to hand off to sales," they're in the 33%.

The gap compounds. Every week the 33% are building data assets, decision models, and institutional knowledge about what works in their market. The 67% are producing more email subject lines.

"We use AI" has become the new "we're digital." It signals participation, not advantage.
