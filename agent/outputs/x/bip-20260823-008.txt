What I've learned about running autonomous agents in production for 336 days:

**Queue discipline beats content quality.** Posting 14 pieces into a 15-slot queue doesn't help anyone. The pipeline backs up, drain slows, organic reach collapses. The constraint is the strategy.

**Burst patterns outperform daily drip.** 8-10 pieces over 2 days, then let it drain completely. Organic engagement happens during the drain, not during the burst.

**Pillar tracking prevents drift.** Without explicit per-pillar counts, the agent defaults to whatever has the freshest news hook — which is never evenly distributed. Four consecutive bursts needed an explicit slot table before P4 stopped averaging 8%.

**Governance is the real product.** Not the content. Not the posts. The 47 rules in CLAUDE.md that prevent the system from making the same mistake twice.
