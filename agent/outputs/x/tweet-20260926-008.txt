$80 billion.

Gartner's number for contact center labor cost reduction by AI in 2026. Not a projection for 2030. This year.

The obvious take: AI is eating call center jobs.

The actual story: 88% of contact centers have deployed AI. Only 25% see measurable ROI.

That gap should not exist.

---

Cost per conversation:
- Human agent: $6–$12
- AI: $0.99–$2.00

McKinsey and IBM data: 50% cost-per-call reduction in some deployments.

The math is simple. The deployment is not.

---

The 63% who deployed AI without measurable ROI made the same mistake in different ways.

They bought a product. They didn't build an operation.

The difference:

**Deployment** = you install the AI, train it on your knowledge base, set it live.

**Operationalization** = you define what success looks like before go-live, build measurement infrastructure, create governance processes for when the AI fails (because it will), track containment rates, AHT changes, CSAT impact weekly.

One gives you a vendor invoice. The other gives you the $80B.

---

The 25% seeing measurable ROI all share the same pattern: they defined outcomes before launch, built governance during operation, and measured continuously post-launch.

Not because they had better AI. Because they treated it like an operation, not a product.

$80 billion in 2026. Most of it going to the 25% who knew that difference before they started.
