$9.78 million saved annually. Staff reduced from 200 to 60. Payback period: 3.2 months.

That's the National Insurance Corp contact center AI case from January 2026. It's real, it's documented, and it's representative of what the market is actually delivering.

The number I want to focus on is the payback period.

3.2 months is faster than most enterprise SaaS implementations. Faster than most consulting engagements. Faster than most change management programs. The ROI mechanics of conversational AI automation in high-volume call centers are now at a point where the investment pays back before most organizations have finished their rollout.

Per-interaction cost dropped to below $1.00 for fully automated resolutions. Human agent cost is $7-12 per interaction. The delta drives the 65-90% unit cost reduction that makes the math work.

But the 200→60 staff number is where the conversation gets harder.

The organizations capturing the fastest payback are the ones that planned workforce transition BEFORE the deployment — not after the ROI materialized. When you automate 80% of inbound volume, you have 140 agents whose roles need to shift: toward escalations, complex empathy-required interactions, relationship calls that AI consistently underperforms on.

The organizations that go from 200→60 cleanly are the ones that spent 6 months defining what those 60 do. The ones that go from 200→60 messily are the ones that let the technology dictate the transition.

$9.78M/year is a ceiling. The floor is determined by what you defined for the 60 people who remained.
