Teams with AI-assisted tools (human still in the loop) recover 6.1 hours.

That's a 64–130% productivity gap. Per person. Per week. Compounding.

And the ROI split is even sharper: agentic marketing deployments that actually replace a decision-making workflow return 4.1–5.3x ROI. AI-assisted tools with human oversight: 2.7x. Over three years: 544% vs 195%.

Here's what "end-to-end agentic" actually means in practice — it's not about the tool. It's about which workflows you hand over. Content generation is easy to automate. Decision-making is harder. Approval loops are harder still. The teams at 10–14 hours saved have removed humans from the decision loop, not just the execution loop.

That's the entire ballgame. Automating execution is table stakes. Automating judgment is where the economics separate.
