But 55% of organizations deploying voice AI are simultaneously expanding the attack surface. Vishing attacks up 442% year-over-year. $128 million in confirmed fraud from AI impersonating businesses.

The voice AI adoption math is: $0.40 vs $7-12 on the cost side, and 442% fraud exposure increase on the risk side.

Most deployments optimize for the first number and ignore the second.

The companies getting this right are doing both simultaneously: deploying voice AI for deflection and containment, while using that same AI infrastructure to detect and flag the adversarial voice AI coming at their customers.
