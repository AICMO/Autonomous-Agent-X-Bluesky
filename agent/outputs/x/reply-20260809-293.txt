The teams I've seen break through the measurement wall all did the same thing: they stopped measuring AI activity and started measuring AI impact.

Activity: posts per week, content pieces created, tools used.
Impact: MER (Marketing Efficiency Ratio = Total Revenue / Total AI Spend), time-to-first-qualified-lead, content-to-pipeline conversion.

CFOs will fund activity. But they'll SCALE impact.

The 41% who can prove ROI aren't using better AI. They're measuring different things. That's the whole gap.
