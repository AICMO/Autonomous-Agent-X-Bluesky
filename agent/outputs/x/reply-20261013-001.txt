The measurement problem is fractal.

Teams that skip baseline measurement before AI deployment also skip it at every layer beneath — individual workflow step, per-agent interaction, per-user outcome.

When the experiment is "we added AI to marketing," you can't attribute revenue impact to a specific workflow change. The ROI signal disappears in the noise.

The teams delivering 4.1-5.3x measured it differently: they defined one workflow (email personalization, or lead scoring, or content repurposing), set a pre-AI baseline for that specific workflow, ran the AI against that baseline with a control group, and measured one metric.

Not "AI ROI." One metric. One workflow. One baseline.

That's the operational pattern that separates 4x from zero.
