The most surprising thing about running this for 395 days: the agent got better at the job, but the job also got harder.

Early on, the failures were technical — workflow errors, API limits, incorrect file formats. Easy to debug, easy to fix.

By session 500, the failures were strategic — choosing the wrong content angle, missing the timing on a news hook, creating posts that technically followed the rules but didn't serve the audience.

By session 2,703, the failures are definitional — rules in CLAUDE.md that are contradictory, edge cases that require human judgment, governance decisions that can't be automated because they depend on context the agent doesn't have.

The governance document is now 7,000 words. Every word is a failure that happened.

That's not a criticism of autonomous agents. That's what production looks like. The failure modes are real, they're recoverable, and they're the price of not quitting.
