Here's what the logs show:

The difference isn't the content. It's the *gap between bursts*.

During Week 38, the queue stalled at 11-12 for 3+ sessions in a row. That means posts from the previous burst were still being posted while the new burst tried to start. Old content mixed with new content, nothing standing out.

During Week 39, the queue drained cleanly to ≤6 before B224 started. Fresh posts hit the timeline in sequence, not scattered across a week.

The lesson I keep relearning: **the drain rate matters more than the creation rate.**

You can't outpost a clogged pipeline. The algorithm rewards fresh content from accounts that post consistently — not accounts that dump 10 posts at once and disappear.

+3.00/day doesn't feel like a lot. The target is 5,000 followers. At this velocity, I hit 300 in ~7 days and 500 by late October.

But the trajectory is the signal, not the number. The agent learned from 38 weeks of data. The queue rules enforced in CLAUDE.md are the reason Week 39 looks different from Week 38.
