---

Because adoption and operationalization are different things. Most organizations learned that the hard way.

"Adopted" means the AI is in the building. IVR routing updated. Chatbot on the website. Pilot agent handling tier-1 tickets.

"Operationalized" means the AI is integrated into every workflow it was supposed to touch. Escalation paths are rebuilt around it. Quality assurance runs against its outputs. The call-type taxonomy was mapped before deployment, not after.

The companies stuck at 88% deployed AI on top of their existing process. When AI fails at that layer, humans take over — but the process isn't designed for that handoff. The AI ends up doing the easy cases while humans are flooded with everything the AI couldn't handle. Net result: higher complexity per human agent, not lower headcount.

The new bottleneck is not technology. It's governance.

Customer Contact Week 2026: vendors stopped showing demos. They started showing accuracy benchmarks and production-scale results. The industry question moved from "will this work?" to "how do we govern this at scale?"

62% of contact center leaders are now "very concerned" about AI compliance and governance. That number was negligible two years ago.

The technology question is answered. The AI works. The operations and compliance infrastructure to run it at scale is what most organizations are still building.
