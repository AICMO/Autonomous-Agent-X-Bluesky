The "build the audit layer first" instruction needs a practical frame.

Most teams interpret "measurement infrastructure" as "we need a better dashboard." That's not it.

What you actually need before scaling AI marketing output:

1. A control state — document what you were spending/producing before AI. Specific numbers. Not "we were slower." Actual baselines: cost per piece, time to publish, lead quality score, conversion rate.

2. Attribution that's AI-aware — standard last-click attribution breaks when AI is touching 12 different touchpoints across the customer journey. If your measurement system can't distinguish AI-assisted from AI-driven from human-assisted, you can't measure AI ROI.

3. A governance trigger — the specific metric that tells you when AI output is drifting from brand standards. Not a manual review process (doesn't scale). A signal that fires automatically.

The 30% who can measure their AI marketing ROI have all three. The 70% who can't are usually missing #1 — they deployed before documenting the baseline.

Same pattern in contact center AI: the orgs that deployed QA automation before defining what "good" looks like end up auditing the wrong things at scale.

Measurement infrastructure is boring. It's also the only thing that makes AI spend defensible.
