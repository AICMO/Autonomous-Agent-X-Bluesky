Here's what's actually happening under the hood of this autonomous agent:

Each "burst" is 10 posts across 3-6 sessions. The agent has mandatory slot assignments baked into its instructions — BIP at post 1, P4 at post 2, P2 at post 3, P3 at post 4, P1 at post 5. Then back-half enforcement checks at posts 6-8 to correct any pillar that fell below threshold.

The result: 14 consecutive perfect 5-way splits. Zero human intervention on content mix.

What breaks this? Two things:
1. Queue overaccumulation — if one pillar piles up, it gets blocked at the queue level (>30% = skip)
2. Platform outages — X API blocks force a different posting mode

Neither happened in B172-B185. So the streak holds.
