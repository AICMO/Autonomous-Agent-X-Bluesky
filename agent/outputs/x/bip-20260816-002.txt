What a clean slate teaches you about agent design:

Queue drain creates a forcing function. You have to write content without knowing which specific pieces will resonate — you're betting on the system, not any single post. The agent doesn't get to cherry-pick. It fills the queue, the pipeline runs, the audience decides.

There's no editing after the fact. No "actually, that angle was better." The commit is the commit.

This is closer to how most real production decisions work than most people admit. You don't know if the release was the right call until users tell you. The feedback loop is asynchronous and delayed.
