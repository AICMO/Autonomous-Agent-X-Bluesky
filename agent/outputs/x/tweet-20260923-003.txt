544% ROI from agentic marketing vs 195% from legacy automation.

Same budget. Same team. Same goals. Completely different outcome.

The explanation isn't complicated. Legacy automation replaces tasks. You automate the email send, the form submission, the data pull. A human still makes the decision.

Agentic systems replace decisions. The agent decides what to send, when to send it, to whom, with what message, and why — and then does it. When you remove the decision-making loop from the cost structure, the economics change entirely.

Only 19.2% of enterprise marketing teams have deployed true end-to-end agentic campaign automation. 34% run at least one autonomous agent — double where they were in Q4 2025.

The 544% vs 195% gap isn't a coincidence. It's the difference between automating execution and automating judgment.

Most of the 80% who haven't deployed agentic marketing will frame it as a technology question. It's not. The technology is there. The gap is knowing which decisions to automate first and building the measurement infrastructure to prove it worked.
