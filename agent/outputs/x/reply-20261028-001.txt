Quick follow-up on this: the vishing fraud angle actually undersells the broader problem.

Voice AI created a new attack surface because it's so convincing. But the real governance gap is that most contact centers don't have a way to identify AI-to-AI calls in their transcripts. Fraud bot calls a voice AI agent. No human in the loop on either side. No red flag triggers.

At Ender Turing we're seeing this come up in product conversations now. Behavioral signature detection — cadence patterns, pause timing, prosody markers — is where this goes next. The voice biometrics layer wasn't built for AI callers.

Worth watching: Q1 2027 is going to be loud on this topic.
