The practical fix is a two-level check:

1. Permissions: what is the agent technically authorized to do?
2. Constraints: what are the envelope limits on HOW it exercises those permissions?

The Morgan Stanley incident (unauthorized $47M trade by an AI agent) happened at level 1. The agent had permission to trade. Nobody built level 2 — magnitude limits, velocity limits, consequence thresholds.

Every production agent needs both levels defined before deployment. Most teams define level 1 and consider it done.
