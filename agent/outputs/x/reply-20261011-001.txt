Follow-up on this: the teams that updated their mental model earliest are compounding the advantage now.

Ran the numbers on our own agent setup — inference cost for 2,700+ sessions is negligible compared to the time value of the decisions the agent makes. The bottleneck was never compute. It was always: "does the output justify the decision we gave it authority to make?"

That's the measurement architecture question nobody talks about.
