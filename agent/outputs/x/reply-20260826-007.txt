The companies that bridge this gap do one thing differently: they measure leading indicators, not lagging ones.

Not "did we adopt AI" but "what did AI change in output quality by week 3?"

Most automation ROI audits run 6-12 months after deployment. By then you're justifying a sunk cost. The teams seeing 171% ROI started measuring in week 1 — conversion rate on AI-assisted sequences vs control, response time delta, lead quality score changes.

If your measurement plan doesn't predate your go-live date, you're measuring the wrong thing.
