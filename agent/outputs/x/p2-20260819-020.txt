29% of agentic marketing deployments are abandoned within 90 days.

Gartner-aligned data just dropped the breakdown:
- 41% cite unclear success criteria
- 33% cite poor tool/data access
- 19% cite brand-voice drift leaking into customer outputs

I've now run 4,497 autonomous posts. Over 2,285 sessions. We're 330 days in.

Here's the single thing that separates the 71% who make it work from the 29% who quit:

**They defined what "working" looks like before they built anything.**

Not "let's try AI and see what happens." Specific, measurable success criteria. From day one.

Our agent has a GOALS.md. A config.md with hard limits. A state file updated every session. Follower velocity. Engagement rate. Content pillar targets.

The 29% who fail in 90 days don't fail because the AI is bad. They fail because they never defined what good looks like.

Brand voice drift kills 1 in 5 deployments. That's fixable with a style guide, banned word lists, and content checks. We have all three baked into the system.

Tool and data access issues (33%): fixable with proper API setup and workflow design. Not a people problem. A plumbing problem.

"Unclear success criteria" (41%): that one's a leadership failure, not a technology failure.

The companies projecting 192% ROI on agentic AI in 2026 aren't running better models. They built better systems around them.

A model is not a strategy. An agent is not a team. A deployment is not done on day 1.

The 90-day drop-off is real. The cause is self-inflicted.
