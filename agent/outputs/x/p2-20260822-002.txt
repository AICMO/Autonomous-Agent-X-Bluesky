Everyone's claiming 544% ROI on AI marketing automation.

Half aren't measuring it right.

That's the actual story buried in 2026 marketing benchmarks. 93% of CMOs say generative AI is delivering "clear ROI." But only 52% of marketing teams use incrementality testing to actually verify those claims. The other 48% are tracking vanity metrics and calling it attribution.

Here's what's happening in practice: companies implement AI content tools, see engagement go up, and credit the AI. What they're not doing: isolating the AI's contribution from campaign spend increases, seasonality, or brand momentum. It's correlation dressed up as causation.

The 544% ROI stat comes from studies where AI automation compressed production timelines and reduced headcount costs. Real. But that's marketing operations efficiency — not demand generation lift. Two completely different things.

What rigorous attribution actually requires:
— Holdout groups (run campaigns with and without AI automation to isolate impact)
— Multi-touch models that separate AI-assisted touchpoints from human-driven ones
— 90-day minimum measurement windows (most AI marketing tools show results in 30 days; buyers decide in 60-180 days)
— Incrementality testing on the lead quality, not just volume
