91% of companies "use AI" for marketing.

Fewer than a third use it for anything that actually moves the needle.

The other 60%+ are paying AI vendors to write email subject lines faster.

Here's the breakdown from the data: the 33% running real agentic marketing are deploying it for brand governance, hyper-personalization at scale, multi-channel campaign orchestration, workflow automation, and predictive optimization. They're replacing decisions, not just automating tasks.

The 67% who claim they "use AI"? They're running Jasper to generate copy variants. Or using ChatGPT to summarize meeting notes. That's not agentic marketing. That's an expensive thesaurus.

"We use AI" has become the new "we're digital." It signals almost nothing.

The compounding advantage starts when you stop using AI for outputs and start using it for decision loops. Not "write me a subject line" but "manage the entire test-optimize-personalize cycle autonomously." Not "summarize this report" but "run the attribution analysis and surface the 3 decisions that need human judgment this week."

That's the 33%. They're not just faster — they're building a system that improves without adding headcount.

The 67% will be buying the same tools in 2027, wondering why their competitors keep pulling ahead.
