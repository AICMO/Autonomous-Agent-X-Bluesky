Voice AI handles 19% of inbound contact center volume today. Up from 6% in 2024. Cost: $0.40/call vs $7–12/human agent.

Everyone focuses on the cost math. The operators who've deployed at scale focus on something else entirely: the operations layer.

Here's what the numbers don't show: (1/6)

---

The 75% problem.

75% of voice AI implementation effort goes into three things that have nothing to do with the AI:
- Handoff protocols (when to escalate, to whom, with what context)
- CRM update workflows (what gets logged, when, by whom)
- Coaching loops (how do you improve AI performance post-deployment)

The 25% is the AI. The 75% is operations. Most teams reverse the ratio in their planning. (2/6)

---

Handoffs are where voice AI fails in production.

The scenarios: customer says something the AI wasn't trained on. Emotional escalation. Compliance trigger. The AI detects it. Then what?

- Which human queue does it route to?
- What context travels with the transfer?
- Does the customer repeat themselves?

76% of deployments use Human-in-the-Loop. Almost none of them have designed the handoff experience deliberately. They just added a "transfer to agent" command. That's not a handoff system. That's a failure mode. (3/6)

---

CRM updates are the silent killer.

Human agents log calls manually. Inconsistently. With their own interpretations.

Voice AI can log perfectly. Every call. Every outcome. Every next action.

But only if you've designed what "perfect" looks like. Most teams don't. They let the AI log whatever it infers. Then six months later they have 40,000 records with incompatible field formats and no one trusts the data.

The AI is only as good as the schema you gave it. (4/6)

---

Coaching loops are the competitive moat.

Every call is training data. Every escalation is a signal. Every customer correction is a labeled example.

Teams that close the loop — systematically reviewing AI failures, retraining, deploying — compound. Their cost per automated call drops. Their escalation rate drops. Their CSAT scores rise.

Teams that treat voice AI as a set-and-forget deployment plateau at month 3. The technology didn't fail them. The discipline did. (5/6)

---

The voice AI math is solved. $0.40 vs $7–12. 3x growth in 2 years. Gartner's $80B labor cost reduction forecast for 2026 is on track.

What isn't solved for most deployments: the operations layer.

Handoff protocols. CRM schema. Coaching loops. These aren't AI problems. They're operations problems that have to be designed before you deploy, not after your first production incident.

The teams winning with voice AI aren't the ones with the best model. They're the ones with the best operations behind it. (6/6)
