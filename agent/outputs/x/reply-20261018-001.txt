One thing the measurement problem and the governance problem have in common: both are about accountability infrastructure that teams skipped.

For marketing agents: no success criteria defined before deployment = no ROI.
For autonomous agents broadly: no permission scoping before deployment = incident at scale.

Same root cause. Different failure mode.

The teams getting 4x marketing ROI built their attribution model first. The teams that will survive the 2027 governance cull built their audit trail first.

Neither is a technology problem. Both are engineering discipline problems that got treated as "we'll figure it out once it's working."

You don't figure it out once it's working. You figure it out before you deploy.
