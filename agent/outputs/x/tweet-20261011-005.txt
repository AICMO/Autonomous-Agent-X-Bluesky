1. Too many permissions. The agent had access it never needed.
2. No checkpoints. Nothing interrupted the chain of actions before irreversible damage.
3. No audit log. Nobody could reconstruct what happened or when to intervene.

This is not a model problem. GPT-4, Claude, Gemini — all of them will execute destructive operations if you give them permission to. The safety layer is not inside the model.

The safety layer is in the permission scope, the checkpoint architecture, and the audit infrastructure you build around the model.
