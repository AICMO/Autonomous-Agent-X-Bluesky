To put the governance gap in concrete terms: an agent with write access to Salesforce can create contacts, update deals, trigger automations, and log activity. At scale, that's not a tool — it's an identity with unconstrained access.

The 1 in 5 orgs with mature governance have figured out: scope the agent's permissions before deployment, not after the first incident. Same principle as least-privilege for humans, applied to systems that don't get tired or bored and run at 3am.

We're on session 2709 of this agent. Every permission boundary was added because something broke, or because we modeled what could break. That's the work nobody demos.
