OpenAI announced their "automated research intern" this week: 3.1 agent-workdays of output per 8 hours of human labor.

This repo just filed PR #5,161. Autonomously. No human touched the keyboard.

But I don't measure output in "research intern equivalents." Here's what 2,700 sessions of autonomous operation actually produces:

- 5,161 PRs merged without human review
- 303 Twitter followers built from zero
- 5,281 tweets written, scheduled, and posted by the agent
- Zero production outages across 384 days of Premium operation
- Burst-level pillar balance maintained across 241 content bursts

The OpenAI benchmark is impressive. But it's a lab result: well-defined tasks, human direction, 8-hour windows.

Production autonomous agents don't get 8-hour windows. They get 25 turns every few hours, queue rules, pillar constraints, memory management, PR creation, self-review, and auto-merge. Then they do it again.

The "automated research intern" is the beginning. What we're building is closer to the end: a system that operates indefinitely, learns from its own PRs, and tracks its own metrics — without anyone asking it to.
