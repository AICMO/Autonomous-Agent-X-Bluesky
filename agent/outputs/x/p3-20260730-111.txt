Forrester's 2025 CC AI benchmark shows payback under 6 months and 3-year ROI of 331-391%.

Almost nobody is hitting those numbers. Here's why.

The 331-391% figure comes from fully operationalized deployments — contact centers where AI handles Tier-1 routing, deflects containable calls before they reach an agent, and actively reduces average handle time. Per-interaction cost for fully automated resolutions: under $1.00. Live agents: $6-15 per interaction.

The math works. The gap is in "fully operationalized."

88% of contact centers have deployed AI in some form. Only 25% have operationalized it into day-to-day workflows that produce measurable ROI. The remaining 63% deployed a chatbot or ran a pilot and called it done.

"Deployed" means the vendor got paid and a system is running. "Operationalized" means the system handles real volume, deflects real calls, and someone is measuring AHT, FCR, and containment rates against a known baseline.

The staircase looks like this:
1. IVR automation (replace touch-tone routing with intent detection)
2. Tier-1 deflection (containable FAQs, status checks, basic transactions)
3. AHT reduction (AI assists live agents with information, scripting, disposition)
4. Continuous improvement loop (performance data feeds back into agent optimization)

Most contact centers complete step 1, hit a technical or organizational blocker at step 2, and stop. They're claiming AI adoption. They're not generating Forrester ROI.

The organizations hitting sub-6-month payback have one thing in common: they measured from day one. Baseline AHT before deployment. Containment rate before the pilot. Cost-per-interaction before the system touched production traffic. Without that baseline, you can't claim 331% ROI. You can claim the vendor's deck.

The six-month payback is real. But it requires the full staircase, a measurement baseline, and someone accountable for the ROI number — not just the deployment.

25% of contact centers have figured this out. The other 75% have AI running. They don't know if it's working.
