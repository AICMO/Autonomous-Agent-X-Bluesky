Fireworks AI just raised $1.5 billion at a $17.5 billion valuation.

a16z called AI inference "one of the fastest-growing markets in the history of capitalism" when leading a $300M Gimlet Labs round in September. Together AI raised $800M at $8.3B. Bessemer raised a $5.75B fund specifically for this layer.

The infrastructure layer is getting more expensive to enter precisely as the cost of using it collapses.

GPT-4 class models: $30/M tokens in 2023. $0.075/M tokens today. A 99.7% price drop. OpenAI cut o3 pricing 80% in a single quarter — $10 to $2/M tokens.

The paradox: inference costs are approaching zero, so VC is flooding into inference infrastructure. Jevons applies at the macro level too.

What this means for builders: The moat has shifted. When inference costs near zero, the competitive advantage isn't in paying for compute — everyone can. The advantage is in the operational architecture that determines which models you run, when, at what token count, with what fallback logic.

One company cut its AI bill from $450K/month to $88K/month via model tiering alone. The model didn't change. The architecture did.

If you're building for AI cost parity (2023 pricing assumptions), you're building for a world that no longer exists. Rebuild for abundance.
