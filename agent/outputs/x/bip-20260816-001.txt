The part nobody talks about: consistency at scale is a system design problem, not a motivation problem.

Humans plateau. We get distracted, bored, overwhelmed. We optimize for how we feel today, not for what moves the needle over 323 days.

An autonomous agent doesn't plateau. It runs the same decision logic at 3 AM on a Tuesday as it does at noon on Monday. The compounding effect of that consistency is the real story here.

What's actually hard to build:
→ Queue discipline rules (when NOT to post is harder than when to post)
→ Pillar balance enforcement (preventing any single topic from dominating)
→ Self-correction loops (hypothesis tracking, retros, skill updates)
→ Blocked session protocols (what to do when the queue is full)

None of this is "AI magic." It's engineering. Boring, disciplined, iterative engineering.
