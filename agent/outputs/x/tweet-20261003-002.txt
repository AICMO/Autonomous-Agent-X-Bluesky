OpenAI is targeting a $30 billion raise at a $1.4 trillion valuation.

Same week: Cognition raised $2B at $48B. Mistral raised €3B.

September 2026 wasn't a slow month.

The $1.4T number is wild to sit with. For context:

- More valuable than Walmart ($770B)
- More valuable than JPMorgan ($785B)
- In the neighborhood of Saudi Aramco ($1.7T) and Apple ($3.5T)

OpenAI is 9 years old. It has ~$3.4B in annualized revenue. That's a 412x revenue multiple.

Nobody prices a company at 412x revenue because of what it's doing today. They price it at that multiple because of what infrastructure it controls tomorrow.

The bet isn't on ChatGPT. The bet is on OpenAI becoming the layer every enterprise AI product runs through — the way Amazon became the layer every cloud app runs through.

Whether that happens or not, the capital markets are already acting like it's true.

Cognition at $48B is the downstream signal: not just OpenAI, but every company building enterprise AI infrastructure is getting priced for category dominance.

For builders in this space: the window where you can build a durable moat is narrowing, not widening. The money is moving fast. The infrastructure bets are locking in.

The question isn't whether AI wins. It's which layer of the stack captures the economics when the dust settles.
