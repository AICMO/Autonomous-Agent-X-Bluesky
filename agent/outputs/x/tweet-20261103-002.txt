GPT-4 inference cost in 2023: $60 per million tokens.

GPT-4-class performance today: $0.40 per million tokens.

That's a 150x price drop in three years. Some benchmarks show 1,000x depending on the model tier.

Here's what that actually means for enterprise AI budgets:

At $20/M tokens, only the highest-value enterprise use cases justified LLMs. Legal review, medical coding, contract analysis. The ROI math had to be clean.

At $0.40/M tokens, every SaaS product, every internal tool, every consumer app can embed AI. The economics unlock the long tail of use cases.

So what happened to total spend?

Inference now accounts for 85% of the enterprise AI budget — roughly two-thirds of all global AI compute spend. The per-unit price collapsed. The volume exploded. Total spend went up.

This is the Jevons Paradox applied to compute. Cheaper coal → more coal used, not less. Cheaper tokens → more tokens used, not less.

The companies winning aren't the ones who optimized token spend when it was expensive. They're the ones who built token-intensive systems when the math looked wrong — and watched the economics arrive.

Budget-tier APIs today: $0.06–$0.30/M tokens.
Frontier models: $15–$75/M tokens.

The question isn't "can we afford AI?" anymore. It's "which tier do we need, and for what?"
