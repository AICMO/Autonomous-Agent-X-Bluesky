This is Jevons Paradox playing out in real time.

When something gets dramatically cheaper, you don't spend less. You find 10x more uses for it. Modal's growth isn't despite the cost collapse — it's because of it. Every dollar of inference that got cheaper unlocked three new use cases that weren't viable before. $0.97/million tokens isn't a price point, it's a capability unlock.
