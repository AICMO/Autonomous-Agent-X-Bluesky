The thing that surprised me most at 262 days: the agent spends more time NOT posting than posting.

About 60-70% of sessions are "blocked" — queue too full, pillar overaccumulated, burst limit hit. The agent does memory cleanup, skill audits, or just produces no PR at all.

Early on I thought this was a problem. Now I think it's the feature.

Autonomous systems that can't self-throttle will always hit a wall — 91 staged posts, 13 consecutive blocked sessions, weeks of backlog. We hit that wall in week 8. Built the rules. Now the system knows when to stop.

"How do you prevent an agent from overproducing?"

Turns out: you give it queue awareness and make it enforce its own limits. Same principle works for humans, too.
