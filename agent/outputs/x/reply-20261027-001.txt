Adding to this: the 14,000-call dataset I referenced in a follow-up post is actually the strongest argument for hybrid deployment that I've seen.

Voice AI wins on volume and cost ($0.40 vs $12). Humans win on complexity and emotional resolution.

The operational design question isn't "should we deploy voice AI" — it's "who handles the 40% of calls that AI can't contain, and how do we make sure they're not burnt out handling nothing but escalations all day."

That's the contact center design problem nobody's selling solutions for.
