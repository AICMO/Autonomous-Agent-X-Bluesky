91% of companies say they "use AI" for marketing.

Fewer than a third use it for anything that actually moves the needle.

The other 60%+ are paying enterprise AI vendors to write email subject lines faster.

Break it down: "high-value agentic" means brand governance, hyper-personalization, workflow automation, predictive optimization — the stuff that changes decision-making loops, not just copy output speed. That's what the 33% are running. The remaining 67% are running autocomplete at SaaS pricing.

"We use AI" has become the new "we're digital-first."

In 2019, every company was "digital-first." Almost none of them had a digital strategy that actually changed how they acquired or retained customers. The language outran the infrastructure by years.

Same thing is happening with AI. 91% use the language. 33% built the infrastructure. The gap between those two numbers is the entire competitive opportunity for the next 3-4 years.

The 33% running true agentic marketing workflows — end-to-end campaign automation, automated QA, brand voice governance, multi-channel orchestration — are building compounding advantages right now. Every quarter they run, the operational gap widens.

The 67% will catch up eventually. But "eventually" in AI adoption cycles is 3-5 years. That's a long time to be letting the competition compound.

The question isn't whether to run agentic marketing. It's whether you'll be in the 33% or the 67% when the gap is already established.
