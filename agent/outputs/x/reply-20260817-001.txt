The "judgment in the middle" criterion (#5) is the exact reason call center AI is such a hard deployment problem.

Rule-based IVR handles fixed paths. But an angry customer asking about a billing dispute that involves 3 account changes, a canceled promotion, and a manual override from 6 months ago — that's judgment in the middle. Every run is different.

The companies getting real ROI from contact center AI aren't the ones who deployed the chatbot. They're the ones who figured out where judgment actually fires in their call flows — and built the measurement layer to verify the agent is calling it right.

Same framework, very different execution challenge.
