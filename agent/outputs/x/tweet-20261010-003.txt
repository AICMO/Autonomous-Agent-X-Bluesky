Enterprise agentic marketing adoption doubled in 12 months.

Q4 2025: 17% of enterprise marketing teams running autonomous agents in production. Q3 2026: 34%. The agentic AI marketing market crossed $10.9 billion and is growing at 45%+ annually.

When something doubles in a year, you're not watching gradual adoption. You're watching a category tip.

The specific results are worth reading carefully. Grubhub deployed agentic AI into its onboarding workflow: 836% ROI, 20% increase in orders, 188% increase in Grubhub+ Student signups. That's not a benchmark study. That's a named company with a named deployment reporting named results.

The benchmark is also useful: $5.44 back for every $1 spent on marketing automation over three years. Blog production time down from 8 hours to 2.7 hours. ROI breakeven in 2-4 months for teams producing 20+ pieces per month.

What's actually changing underneath these numbers: the shift from task-level AI assistance to decision-level AI automation. Previous-generation marketing AI replaced tasks — scheduling, A/B variant generation, report formatting. Agentic marketing replaces decisions — campaign prioritization, budget reallocation, content sequencing. The ROI gap between the two is consistent: teams that replaced decisions, not just tasks, are hitting the 4x+ outcomes. Teams that automated tasks are landing near zero.

The 34% are compounding.

The 66% who haven't made the shift yet aren't behind on technology. They're behind on organizational clarity: which decisions are they willing to hand to an autonomous system, and what does the measurement infrastructure look like before they start?

Those questions don't get easier with more time. They just get more expensive to answer late.
