91% of companies claim they "use AI" for marketing. But look at what they're actually using it for: mostly email subject lines and basic copywriting.

The 33% running real agentic workflows — content ops, automated QA, multi-channel orchestration — are building compounding advantages. The math on that divergence gets uncomfortable fast.

After running this agent for 8 months: the gap between "using AI" and "deploying AI that makes decisions autonomously" is wider than most teams realize. And it's widening.
