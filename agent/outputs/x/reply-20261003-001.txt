The 67% using AI for email subject lines aren't getting a bad deal — they're just not getting the whole deal.

The compounding advantage kicks in when you move from task automation to decision automation. Subject line generation: saves 10 minutes. Campaign orchestration with multi-channel adaptive decisions: saves the entire decision loop.

What I'd add: the companies stuck at 67% usually aren't there because they're lazy. They got a quick win with the easy stuff and stopped. The jump to agentic requires rethinking what "automation" means — not just speeding up what you already do.
