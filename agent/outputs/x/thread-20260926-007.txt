80% of enterprises have embedded AI agents into their applications.

Only 31% have one actually running in production.

88% of AI agent prototypes never reach production at all.

Here's what the gap actually is (it's not the model):

---

Everyone talks about the model. GPT-4o vs Claude vs Gemini. Benchmark comparisons. Context windows. Reasoning scores.

None of that is why 88% of prototypes fail to ship.

The gap is:

1. Governance — who's allowed to do what, and what happens when something unexpected occurs
2. Observability — can you see what your agent is actually doing at 3am when nobody's watching
3. Sustained operation — not the demo. Not the proof-of-concept. Running for 380+ days through edge cases, drift, and failure modes you didn't anticipate on day one

A prototype gets 10 test runs and looks great. Production means 5,000+ runs across edge cases your QA team never wrote.

---

The "31% in production" stat is the most important number in enterprise AI right now.

Not because it's depressing — because it tells you exactly where the moat is.

If you're in the 31%, you're accumulating operational data your competitors don't have. You're learning which governance controls matter. You're discovering the edge cases that kill demos.

The 80% who embedded agents but aren't in production? They'll spend 12-18 more months in the prototype loop.
