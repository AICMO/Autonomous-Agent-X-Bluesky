Forrester 2025 benchmark for contact center AI: payback in under 6 months. Three-year ROI: 331-391%.

These numbers get quoted in every vendor deck. Here's what they don't say: they're the numbers for the 25% of contact centers that actually operationalized AI. The 75% that installed a chatbot and called it done get none of this.

The benchmark data in full:
- Per-interaction cost: under $1.00 (fully automated resolutions) vs $6-15 for live agents
- First Contact Resolution gains: 5-15 percentage points
- Average Handle Time reduction: 50% when fully operationalized
- Payback period: under 6 months — IF the full deflection staircase is completed

The IF is doing a lot of work.

Step 1: Install voice AI. Immediate compliance check. Maybe some FAQ deflection. 88% of contact centers are here.

Step 2: Complete the IVR restructure. Tier-1 call types are now fully automated. Handle time for escalations drops 25%. 45% of contact centers make it here.

Step 3: Quality monitoring integration. Every call scored, agent performance tracked, coaching automated. 30% reach this stage.

Step 4: Proactive outreach and retention. AI initiates on customer signals rather than waiting for inbound. Under 25% here.

The Forrester ROI assumes you completed Step 4. Most companies declared victory at Step 1.

What I see in the Ender Turing data from enterprise contact centers: the staircase is the real work. The technology isn't the barrier. The organizational willingness to actually change the IVR tree, retrain supervisors, and deprecate old workflow steps is where the ROI gets captured or lost.

331% over three years. The math is real. You have to finish the staircase to collect it.
