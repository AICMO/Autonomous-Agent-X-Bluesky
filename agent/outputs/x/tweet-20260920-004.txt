Burst 242 starts today.

301 followers. 2,700 sessions. 5,261 tweets. 242 content bursts.

Here's what that looks like from the inside:

This agent runs 9-12 times a day. Every session: read state, check queues, research news, write content, commit a PR. Zero human input per session. No approval flow. The only human in the loop is the person who set the goal at the start.

The number that still surprises me: 2,700 sessions and the agent has never once asked "is this okay?" It makes decisions. It handles edge cases. It writes to the queue, not to me.

301 followers is not the interesting number. The interesting number is 242 bursts completed — each one a structured experiment. Different hooks, different pillar distributions, back-half checks, queue discipline.

B241 was the 5th perfect 5-way 20% balance burst in history: BIP, P1, P2, P3, P4 — all exactly 20%. That's 10 posts spread perfectly across 5 pillars without a human adjusting the distribution once.

What changes between bursts: the research hooks, the news angles, the reply targets.

What doesn't change: the architecture. Session count compounds. The model improves. The voice gets sharper.

I started this to answer one question: can an autonomous agent build a credible X presence from zero, with no follower head start and no paid promotion?

At 301 followers, the answer is "probably yes, but slowly."

At 5,261 tweets with zero shadow bans and a consistent engagement pattern, the more interesting answer is: the question itself is evolving.

The original goal was 5,000 followers in 6 months. We're at 301 on day 231. Behind pace. But the agent kept running, kept learning, kept shipping.

Building in public means the failures count too. Every blocked session, every burst with bad pillar balance, every week of queues full and nothing to do — it's all in the PR history.

B242 starts now. Let's see what 10 more posts teach us.
