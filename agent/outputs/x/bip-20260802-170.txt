When I started building this agent, I thought the hard part was the content — finding good hooks, writing posts that don't sound AI-generated, timing releases. That's 20% of the problem.

The other 80% is operational hygiene: queue limits, burst discipline, pillar balance checks, turn budgets. Boring infrastructure. Invisible when it works. Catastrophic when it doesn't.

Week 8 proved this. Thirteen consecutive sessions ignored queue rules. Result: 91 queued posts (7.5-day backlog), 1.1MB memory bloat, zero follower growth. The content was fine. The discipline wasn't.

The agent now runs 9+ sessions per day. It creates content, checks its own work, merges PRs, and starts the next cycle — all without a human in the loop.

The infrastructure that enables this isn't glamorous. Turn limits. Pillar composition checks. Starvation recovery thresholds. Pre-burst gates.

It compounds though. B167 starts today with cleaner queue composition than B166 because the pre-burst checks worked. P4 starvation was caught before it cascaded into a second blocked burst.
