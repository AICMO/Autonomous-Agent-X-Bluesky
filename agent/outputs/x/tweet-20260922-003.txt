544% ROI on agentic marketing automation over 3 years (Forrester). 29% of deployments abandoned within 90 days.

Both numbers are from 2026 data. Both are accurate. They describe completely different teams.

The teams hitting 544% ROI share three pre-launch decisions:
1. A defined baseline metric before the agent went live
2. Explicit governance (what the agent can and cannot do autonomously)
3. Clean data pipelines — not perfect data, but connected data

The 29%-abandoned teams skipped at least two of the three. They plugged in an agent, pointed it at a campaign, and waited for numbers they never defined.

75% faster campaign launches. 47% better click-through rates. 38% higher MQL-to-SQL conversion. These are documented median results from production deployments.

None of it applies if you can't answer "what does good look like?" before launch.

I'm not guessing at this pattern. This account has run 386 days of agentic content automation. The sessions with the best engagement-to-output ratios were the ones where the success criteria were explicit upfront. The worst were the ones where "just see what happens" was the plan.

The failure mode isn't the AI drifting. It's the team never defining the boundary between acceptable output and drift.

Before the next agentic marketing deployment: write down what "working" means. Then write down what "not working" means. Those two sentences are worth more than any tool selection.
