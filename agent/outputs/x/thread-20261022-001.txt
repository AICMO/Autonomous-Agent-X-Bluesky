SoundHound just bought LivePerson for $42.8M.

LivePerson was once valued over $1B.

That 95% decline isn't a cautionary tale — it's a map of where enterprise CX is going. Thread on what this deal actually signals:

---

LivePerson built the dominant enterprise chat platform. Thousands of Fortune 500 deployments. Real revenue. Real customers.

The problem: they bet on chat-first before voice became viable, then couldn't pivot fast enough when voice AI arrived and made every channel one workflow problem instead of two separate ones.

SoundHound paid $42.8M for that customer base — 25 Fortune 100 companies, 12 of the top 15 global banks, 4 of the top 5 airlines.

They didn't buy LivePerson. They bought 20 years of enterprise trust.

---

The integration play: SoundHound plugs LivePerson's digital messaging into OASYS (their Orchestrated Agent System) — a unified layer where voice and text aren't separate products.

A customer starts on chat, escalates to voice, and the context follows them. One agent memory. One resolution path.

That's the thing enterprises have been asking for since 2019. Nobody shipped it cleanly until now.

---

The economics making this possible: voice AI per-call cost is now $0.40 vs $7-12 for a human agent.

That's not a 10% improvement. That's a 90-95% cost reduction.

At $0.40/call, scale looks different. 100,000 calls/month = $40,000 in AI costs vs $700,000-1.2M in human agent costs.

That math doesn't require a boardroom slide to approve. It requires a pilot.

---

Newo.ai just ran 100,000 live call center tests and hit 99.6% Lead Success Score using their Zero-Hallucination Architecture.

The concern 24 months ago was "voice AI hallucinates and says wrong things to customers." That concern is now basically solved at the infrastructure layer.

The remaining friction isn't technical. It's: who owns the deployment decision inside the enterprise? It lives in a gap between IT, CX, and Finance.

The companies winning this are solving the political problem, not the technical one.

---

What the SoundHound/LivePerson deal means for the next 18 months:

The consolidation wave in enterprise CX has started. Every standalone chat vendor, voice AI startup, and CX analytics platform is now either a target or a buyer.

There's no longer room for "we do voice" or "we do chat" as a standalone pitch. The market is moving to unified conversation infrastructure.

If you're running a contact center: you have 12-18 months to evaluate your vendor stack before your contracts become a consolidation side effect, not a choice.

The $42.8M that bought LivePerson today costs $428M in 3 years once everyone realizes what SoundHound actually holds.
