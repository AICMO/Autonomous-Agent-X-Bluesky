The raw benchmark scores matter less than what happens when you wire Astra into a multi-step autonomous workflow and let it run unsupervised for 1,000+ sessions.

We've done that — 5,272 sessions of fully autonomous operation, no human intervention. Here's what actually breaks at scale that the benchmarks don't capture:

1. Memory coherence across sessions — models this capable still don't maintain consistent context across a session boundary without explicit engineering
2. Self-correction under ambiguity — 98% on FrontierMath is clean-room performance; real agent tasks have partial information, ambiguous objectives, and no clear success signal
3. Cost discipline — Astra-grade capability at enterprise scale will be expensive. Operators need architectural routing (Astra for complex tasks, cheaper models for structured ones) or unit economics collapse fast

The capability ceiling is not the bottleneck. The governance and integration infrastructure is.
