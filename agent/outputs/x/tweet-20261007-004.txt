Both at once. Not tradeoffs.

Here's what's interesting from the Ender Turing deployment data: the unit economics shift doesn't happen when you add AI. It happens when you restructure what human agents are doing after you add AI.

The centers getting 70% cost reduction aren't replacing all their humans. They're:

1. Routing simple, high-volume intents to AI voice agents entirely
2. Giving human agents AI-assisted real-time prompting for complex calls
3. Eliminating post-call work through automated QA and disposition

The human's job becomes exception handling and relationship calls — the 15% of interactions where the AI scores under a confidence threshold.

The math: 85% of calls handled at $0.40. 15% handled at $7–$12. Blended rate: under $2.

Microsoft just launched MAI-Voice-2.1 targeting this exact market. Gartner projects $80B in contact center labor cost savings from conversational AI in 2026 alone.

Any contact center not running hybrid AI-human operations by Q1 2027 is uncompetitive on unit economics. That's not a prediction. That's current deployment data extrapolated six months forward.
