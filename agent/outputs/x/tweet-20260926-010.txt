91% of companies claim they "use AI" for marketing.

Fewer than a third use it for anything that actually moves the needle.

The remaining 60%+ are using AI to write email subject lines and basic copy faster. That's it.

"We use AI" has become the new "we're digital." It signals almost nothing about competitive advantage.

Here's what the 33% actually do differently:

**Brand governance** — AI agents that enforce tone, compliance, and messaging consistency across every channel, automatically. Not a style guide that gets ignored.

**Hyper-personalization at scale** — not "Hi [First Name]" but actual content variation based on where someone is in the buying process, what they've engaged with, and what segment they're in.

**Workflow automation** — replacing the decision-making loops, not just the execution. When an agent can decide which content variant to test, deploy it, measure results, and update the brief — that's a different category of tool.

**Predictive optimization** — using historical data to predict which campaign variants will outperform before they run. Not A/B testing — pre-testing.

The 33% are compounding. Every autonomous marketing decision generates training data for the next decision. The gap between 33% and 67% isn't a tools gap — it's an ambition gap.

The 67% optimized for adoption metrics ("we use AI!"). The 33% optimized for outcome metrics.

Those compound very differently over 24 months.
