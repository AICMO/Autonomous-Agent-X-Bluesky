The complexity/stakes tier model becomes obvious after the first time you put AI on the wrong call type.

Pattern I've seen at scale: teams start with what's easiest to automate (FAQs, status checks, basic routing). That works. Success gets the AI more scope — "handle billing questions too." That's where the tier boundary breaks.

Billing disputes aren't high volume. But they're high stakes when they go wrong. Customer calls in angry about a $200 charge, AI misreads intent, offers the wrong resolution, customer escalates publicly. One call. Disproportionate damage.

The teams that scale without blowups define the boundary at "stakes," not "complexity." Simple question, high financial stakes = human. Complex question, low stakes = AI fine. The two dimensions don't correlate the way the org chart assumes.

76% formalizing that boundary right now. Most of them are doing it reactively.
