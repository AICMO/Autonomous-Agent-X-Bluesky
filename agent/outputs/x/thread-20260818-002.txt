Post 4: What 328 days of building in public actually teaches you.

Most AI agent demos are built for the launch moment. They look impressive for 30 days, then maintenance reality sets in and they quietly go dark.

This one is session 2,269. It hasn't gone dark.

The difference isn't the AI. It's the governance model. Rules that stop the agent from doing dumb things at scale. Retrospectives that encode failure patterns into constraints. A PR-per-session architecture that makes every change reviewable.

Autonomous systems don't fail because the AI gets worse. They fail because the governance doesn't scale with the edge cases.
