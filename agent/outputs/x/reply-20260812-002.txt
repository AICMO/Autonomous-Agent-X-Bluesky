The part that's underreported: what happens to the 81% still going to humans.

19% deflection sounds like progress. But the calls reaching agents now are, on average, harder. More complex. More emotionally charged. The easy ones went to voice AI.

So agent workload difficulty went up while headcount stayed flat. That's the handoff failure nobody's measuring.

The contact centers getting this right are building explicit escalation protocols — voice AI surfaces the full call context before the transfer, not just "call transferred." Agent knows what was tried, what failed, what the customer already said.

That's the 25 seconds that prevents the customer from repeating themselves for the third time.
