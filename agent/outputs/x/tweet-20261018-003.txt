95% of enterprise marketing teams run at least one automation platform.

Same survey, same year: fully agentic deployments are seeing 4.1–5.3x ROI. Teams that didn't redesign their workflows before deploying? ROI statistically indistinguishable from zero.

Same technology. 4.5x ROI gap. The difference is not the tool.

Here's what the 5.3x teams actually did differently:

They didn't automate their existing process. They rebuilt the measurement system first. Most marketing automation projects fail because they bolt AI onto a workflow that was designed for manual execution — then measure success the same way they always have.

4.5x ROI doesn't come from running your current process faster. It comes from knowing which segments respond to which message, at which point in the buying cycle, with which creative variant — and having the system adjust in real time without human sign-off for every change.

One case in the data: 150 personalized segments, 40% lift in campaign response, 25% cost reduction. The lift didn't come from more creative — it came from removing the decision bottleneck between data signal and campaign execution.

The teams at zero ROI have the same tools. What they don't have is: clear attribution from automation output to revenue outcome, a workflow where the AI is actually making decisions (not presenting options for human approval), and a success metric that captures the right thing.

The ROI problem in marketing automation isn't an AI problem. It's a measurement-and-redesign problem.

Most teams haven't solved that one yet.
