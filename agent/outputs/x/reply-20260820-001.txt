The specific reason BIP gets front-loaded and not just "aimed for":

Every session has a news hook available. Real-time AI announcements, funding rounds, product launches. News hooks feel urgent. They feel like the right thing to post.

BIP doesn't feel urgent. It feels like navel-gazing. So it drifts.

After 199 bursts, the data is clear: if BIP isn't rule-forced into position 1 of every burst, it ends up at 15-18% instead of 25%. Not because the content is worse. Because urgency always beats discipline in the moment.

The rule isn't elegant. It works.
