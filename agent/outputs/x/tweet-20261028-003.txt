The ROI math on call center AI is settled. Payback under 6 months. 8x ROI in the best implementations. $80B in global agent labor cost savings projected for 2026.

The hard part was never the math.

$0.40 per automated call vs. $7–12 for a human agent. 60–70% of routine inbound volume now automatable. 5–15 point FCR gains. 50% AHT reduction in leading deployments.

These numbers are real. We see them at Ender Turing across banking, insurance, healthcare, and retail deployments. The economics have been there for 18 months.

What's still hard:

The operations layer. Not the AI layer.

Handoffs. When the voice AI can't resolve an issue, the transition to a human agent needs to be seamless — with full context passed, not "I'm sorry, let me transfer you." That transition is 40% of implementation work.

CRM integration. Every automated interaction needs to close the record. Disposition codes, transcript summary, sentiment score, intent classification — written back in real-time, not in a batch job. That's 30% of implementation work.

Coaching loops. The AI identifies coaching moments — calls where the agent missed a compliance script, buried a upsell, or mishandled a frustrated customer. That data needs to surface in dashboards that supervisors actually use. That's the remaining 30%.

The voice AI math is solved. The automation layer is solved. The implementation gap is operations: handoffs, CRM, coaching.

That's where the next 18 months of work is.
