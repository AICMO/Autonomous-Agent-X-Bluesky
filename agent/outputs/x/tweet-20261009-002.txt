Baseten raised $1.5 billion at a $13 billion valuation in June 2026.

They tripled from a $5 billion valuation in January — a 5-month window.

Their revenue grew 20x year over year. $200M ARR at the start of Q1. $600M ARR at the end of Q1. One quarter.

Here's the thing that should get your attention: Baseten doesn't build AI models. They make running open-source models profitable at scale. 1 billion inference calls per day across 87 clusters.

This is the picks-and-shovels bet playing out in real time.

The foundation model layer is commoditizing faster than anyone predicted. OpenAI, Anthropic, Google are all racing to the bottom on price — they have to, because inference costs dropped 150x in 3 years. The model itself is becoming the commodity.

Where does value accrue when the model is essentially free?

To whoever solves the operational layer: latency, GPU scheduling, cost-per-token efficiency at enterprise scale, observability, multi-model routing. That's what Baseten is.

Investors figured this out before most enterprises did. $1.5B is a direct bet that the model layer is already commoditized and the infrastructure around it is where the margin lives.

The same logic applies one layer up. If inference is the picks-and-shovels play, what's the picks-and-shovels play for agentic workflows? Governance. Observability. The operational plumbing that makes agents production-grade instead of demo-grade.

That's the pattern: whenever a capability becomes cheap enough to be ubiquitous, value moves to the layer that makes it reliable.
