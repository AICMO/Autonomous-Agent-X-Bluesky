GPT-4 quality inference cost $60 per million output tokens in 2023.

$0.40 today. 150x collapse in three years. Faster than PC microprocessors. Faster than dotcom bandwidth.

Here's what that actually means for businesses building with AI right now.

The cost question — "can we afford to run this?" — is settled. Completely. A multi-step agent task costs $0.10–$1.00 end to end. The only live question is whether the task output generates more than a dollar of value.

That shift changes everything about how you evaluate AI projects.

Old model: feasibility study, infrastructure cost analysis, "is the ROI positive if we run 100 calls/day?"

New model: scope the task, measure the output value, ship it. The compute is a rounding error.

Enterprise AI budgets are already moving this direction. $1.2M/year in 2024. $7M in 2026. 5.8x in two years. That growth isn't companies spending on infrastructure — it's companies deciding the ROI question is obvious enough to act on.

The teams still doing cost-per-query feasibility studies for automation projects are optimizing for the wrong constraint. The inference layer is solved.

The question is execution quality, measurement precision, and workflow integration. Those still take real work.
