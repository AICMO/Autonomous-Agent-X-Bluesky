Intermountain Health deployed AI voice assistants. Results:

- 85% drop in call abandonment
- 79% improvement in response time

That's not a vendor demo. That's a major US health system with 33 hospitals and 400+ clinics reporting live outcomes from production deployment.

The Bangkok Electronics Retailer case is similar: 86% reduction in wait time, 74% drop in abandonment, 45% CSAT increase.

These numbers come from the same period when the industry was still debating whether AI could "really" work in customer-facing voice interactions.

The answer is in the data. What's harder to talk about: the 75% of contact centers that deployed AI in some form and saw none of these results.

The difference isn't the vendor or the model. It's what happens before go-live:

- Defined success criteria (not "reduce costs" — specific metrics with baselines)
- Governance framework during operation (who reviews edge cases? what escalates to human?)
- Measurement infrastructure post-launch (you need a way to see the number change)

Ender Turing sees this in every deployment. The organizations that hit the Intermountain numbers had all three in place before the first call.

The ones that didn't are in the 75%.
