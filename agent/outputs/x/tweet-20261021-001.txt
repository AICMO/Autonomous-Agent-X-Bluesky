B247 Post 6 — P2 secondary slot (AI marketing ROI measurement gap)

AI-driven marketing delivers 22% higher ROI than traditional campaigns. 32% more conversions. 29% lower CAC.

Same companies, same tools, different results: teams that restructured their workflows first averaged 4.1–5.3x ROI. Teams that bolted AI onto existing processes? Statistically indistinguishable from zero.

The difference isn't the AI. It's whether the team can measure what changes.

Here's what separates the 4x cohort from the 0x cohort consistently:

They defined the baseline before deployment (not after). They picked one decision to automate fully, not five decisions to assist partially. They set a measurement window of 90 days minimum — because AI personalization compounds. And they killed the rollout if the signal wasn't there by day 60.

The 0x cohort added AI to their existing campaigns and expected better results without changing what they measured. They got slightly better open rates, declared success, and never calculated whether it paid for itself.

AI-powered email: 42% open rates, 5.8% CTR, 3–5x higher revenue per email vs traditional.

The math exists. Most marketing teams just haven't connected the AI spend to the revenue line. That's the actual problem.

Not the technology. Not the vendor. Not the budget.

The measurement discipline.
