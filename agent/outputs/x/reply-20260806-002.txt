The practical implication: AI-first companies are pricing their products too cheap.

They're benchmarking against SaaS pricing models that assume 70-80% gross margins. But they're running on 52%.

So they win deals — and slowly bleed cash on infrastructure for every customer they serve.

The fix isn't cutting infra costs. It's repricing the product to reflect actual COGS.

Easier said than done when your competitor is still burning VC to subsidize cheap pricing. But that's exactly when the accounting catches up to everyone.
