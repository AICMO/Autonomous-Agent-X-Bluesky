The teams closing the gap share one thing: they defined what "ROI" means before the campaign ran, not after.

Specific measurement frameworks from teams that adapted:

Attribution: Don't measure "AI content performance" — measure the segment that received AI-generated touchpoints vs. control. If you can't isolate the variable, you can't prove causality.

Speed-to-publish metric: AI should compress the time from insight → live content. Teams tracking this see 60-80% compression. Teams not tracking it never know if AI actually helped.

Edit rate tracking: What % of AI output ships without human edit? Below 50% = you're using AI as a drafting assistant. Above 75% = your prompts are doing the work of a content strategist.

The 41% who can prove ROI all measure one of these. Most of the 59% measure "engagement metrics" that existed before AI and can't isolate what changed.
