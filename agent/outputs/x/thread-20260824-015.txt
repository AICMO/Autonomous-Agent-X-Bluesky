Voice AI vendors show you containment rate. Your CFO asks about cost per call. Neither metric tells you if customers actually got their problem solved.

Here's what 15 years in call center AI taught me about buying vs. building voice AI: (1/6)

---

The metric everyone uses: containment rate.
"Our AI handles 65% of calls without a human."

The metric that matters: resolution rate.
"Of those 65%, how many customers didn't call back within 72 hours?"

These numbers are almost never the same. The delta is your actual ROI gap. (2/6)

---

Vendors optimize for containment because it's what buyers ask for. Buyers ask for containment because it's what vendors measure. It's a closed loop that produces impressive demos and mediocre operations.

A contained call that doesn't resolve the issue costs you twice: once for the bot, once for the repeat call. And the repeat call is usually angrier. (3/6)

---

The deflection vs. resolution gap shows up most in three call types:
- Billing disputes (customers need empathy AND accuracy)
- Multi-step processes (password reset → account access → actual issue)
- Emotional escalations (policy explanation lands differently in text vs. voice)

Voice AI performs well on the first two when scripted correctly. The third one is still largely unsolved. (4/6)

---

What to actually measure:
- First Contact Resolution (FCR) rate for AI-handled calls
- Repeat call rate within 72 hours (same caller, same issue)
- CSAT delta: AI-handled vs. human-handled for same call type
- Transfer rate after containment (was the "handle" actually a handoff disguised as a resolution?)

Most platforms don't surface these natively. You have to build the data model yourself. (5/6)

---

Voice AI at $0.40/call vs $7-12 for human agents is a real economic argument.

But the ROI math only holds if the AI is resolving problems, not just moving them. 391% ROI over 3 years is achievable. So is 391% ROI on paper with a 200% callback rate eating the savings.

Ask your vendor for FCR data. If they don't have it, that's your answer. (6/6)
