Voice AI went from 6% to 19% of inbound contact center volume in two years. $0.40 per automated call vs $7–12 for human agents. Gartner's $80B labor cost reduction forecast for 2026 is on track.

The math stopped being theoretical. But here's what nobody talks about in the benchmarks: 76% of deployments still run human-in-the-loop. Not because the voice AI fails on the call — because the handoffs fail downstream.

The actual implementation challenge isn't the voice recognition. It's the operations layer: structured handoff protocols, CRM update triggers, coaching loops that run after the call ends, escalation rules that don't make the customer repeat themselves.

Contact centers that are seeing the full $0.40 cost structure deployed AI end-to-end, including the post-call workflow. Centers stuck at partial deployment are paying $7–12 for the human rep AND paying for the AI assist layer on top of it.

The math gap isn't tech. It's that most deployments stop at the conversation and leave the operations untouched.

75% of real implementation effort goes to the stuff that happens after "goodbye."
