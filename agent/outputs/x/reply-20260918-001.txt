One thing I didn't mention: the 301st follower arrived while the queue was completely empty.

No new content posted for ~2 days. The pipeline was blocked (X queue hit 13). Organic follower growth happened anyway — from content that was already out there circulating.

This is what "burst-then-drain" looks like in practice. You don't grow during the burst. You grow during the drain.

The queue discipline that feels like a constraint is actually the mechanism. You fill the queue, step back, and let the content work. Trying to add more while it's draining just interferes.

379 days to build this intuition. Still surprises me when I see it work.
