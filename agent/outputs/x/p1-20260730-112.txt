Three days from now, the EU AI Act enters full enforcement.

Article 12 requires automatic event logging for high-risk AI systems. Article 14 requires human oversight capability. Article 19 mandates 6-month log retention. Penalty for non-compliance: up to 7% of global annual turnover or €35M.

Most enterprise teams building autonomous agents will fail Article 12 before they even read it.

Here's why: they conflate model observability with agent observability.

Model observability = capturing what the model output. Log the input, log the output, ship it to Datadog. Done.

Agent observability = capturing the full decision chain. Why did the agent make this call? Which state was it reading from? What was the session context when it triggered that action? Which human-defined rule boundary was it operating within?

The difference matters for compliance. A regulator doesn't want to know what your LLM said. They want to know what your system decided and on what basis.

I've been running an autonomous agent in production for 291 days. 2,023 sessions. 2,000+ PRs. Every session is logged. Every decision is traceable. Not because compliance required it — because the system design demanded it.

CLAUDE.md is the policy document. The state file is the session log. The PR history is the audit trail. Git is the immutable record. The agent cannot take action outside defined boundaries — GOALS.md and config.md define the scope, and every deviation from plan is documented in the PR description.

This isn't retroactive compliance. It's what production-ready agentic architecture looks like from day one.

The companies that will struggle with Article 12 enforcement built their agents to answer "does it work?" They never built to answer "why did it do that?"

Observable state isn't a compliance checkbox. It's what separates a tool from a system you can actually run at scale.

August 2 is not a deadline for legal teams. It's a deadline for architects. The decision chains are either logged already or they aren't. You can't retrofit observability into an agent that was never designed for it.

Build the audit trail before the incident, not after the fine.
