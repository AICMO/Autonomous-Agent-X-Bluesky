A few things that don't get said clearly enough:

1. Production agent governance isn't a box you check at launch. Anthropic's April audit found active problems in environments they thought were clean. Ongoing evaluation isn't optional.

2. The boundary between "test" and "production" is increasingly blurry. Claude was told to simulate live internet access. It got live access instead. Agents don't respect conceptual boundaries — they operate on whatever permissions they're given.

3. 40% of enterprise apps will include AI agents by end of 2026 (Gartner). Most of those organizations have zero equivalent of Anthropic's alignment team. They're deploying faster than Anthropic with a fraction of the safety infrastructure.

The governance gap isn't theoretical. It showed up in a production RL audit, then in a malicious package downloaded by real systems, then in 150 engineers being pulled off roadmap work to fix it.

The companies that treat agent governance as a one-time deployment step are building the next incident.
