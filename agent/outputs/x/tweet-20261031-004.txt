Gartner projects $80 billion in contact center labor cost savings in 2026.

One number explains almost all of it: a routine call now costs $0.40 with voice AI.

Eighteen months ago, it cost $7 to $12.

67% of Fortune 500 companies are already running production voice AI systems. This isn't a pilot conversation anymore.

But the more interesting shift is what RingCentral announced this week. RingCX now runs AI and human agents as a single unified workforce — same supervisor view, same metrics, bidirectional handoff with full context preserved.

Not "AI handles simple calls, humans handle complex ones." One blended workforce where the routing decision happens mid-conversation based on what the customer actually needs.

The two-tier model we spent 2024 and 2025 debating is already a product.

For contact center operators: the question was never "will AI replace agents?" It was always "what does the transition look like and who manages it?" The answer is: it looks like a combined workforce dashboard, and you manage it the same way you manage humans — with metrics and accountability.
