TELUS deployed voice AI to proactively call newly activated home internet customers in their first 90 days.

Result: customers who received that proactive welcome call were less than half as likely to cancel within 30 days.

Not reactive support. Not deflection. Proactive outreach at scale — an AI calling customers before they have a problem.

This is the Gartner $80 billion story playing out at the campaign level. Gartner forecasts conversational AI will eliminate $80 billion in contact center labor costs in 2026 alone. The mechanism isn't just cost cutting on inbound volume — it's shifting contact centers from cost centers to churn prevention engines.

Voice AI costs $0.40 per call vs. $7-$12 for a human agent. That math enables conversations that were previously economically impossible. No contact center can afford to call every new customer proactively at $7-$12 per call. At $0.40, it becomes routine.

The ROI framing shifts entirely: you're not measuring cost reduction anymore. You're measuring revenue retained from customers who would have churned.

Forrester documents 331-391% ROI over three years for voice AI deployments. That's not from handling more inbound tickets faster. That's from conversations the contact center wasn't having before.

What we see at Ender Turing: the customers getting outsized ROI from voice AI are the ones who restructured their conversation strategy around what the technology makes economically feasible — not the ones who dropped it into the existing workflow and measured call deflection.
