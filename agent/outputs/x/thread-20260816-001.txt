391% ROI from voice AI. Payback in under 6 months. $0.40/call vs $7-12/call for human agents.

These numbers exist. They're real. Forrester measured them.

And yet: 88% of contact centers use AI. Only 25% have fully operationalized it.

That's a 75% operationalization failure rate sitting on top of a proven 391% ROI.

Here's why the measurement gap is the actual problem. (Thread 1/5)

---

The math should be simple: AI costs $0.40/call. Humans cost $10/call. Deploy AI. Keep savings.

It's not simple in practice because cost-per-call is not the metric contact center leaders are measured on.

They're measured on CSAT. First Call Resolution. NPS. Agent attrition. Abandonment rate.

AI improves all of these. But the improvement doesn't show up in the system that budgets for AI.

So the ROI exists and the measurement infrastructure doesn't. (2/5)

---

Here's what the data actually shows from Gartner's 2026 contact center benchmarks:

- 30% reduction in total call volume (AI self-service handles it)
- 25% decrease in average handle time
- 10-20pp improvement in first call resolution
- 20% improvement in customer retention
- 30% improvement in satisfaction scores

Every single one of these is measurable. Most contact centers don't measure them with the precision needed to tie improvement to a specific AI tool. (3/5)

---

The operationalization gap isn't a technology problem. The technology works.

It's a measurement infrastructure problem.

The companies that capture 391% ROI built measurement before deployment, not after. They defined what "success" looks like in their specific system — not Forrester's aggregate study — and instrumented for it before going live.

The 75% who failed to operationalize deployed first and asked "did it work?" six months later.

Wrong order. (4/5)

---

If you're running contact center AI and haven't fully operationalized it, the gap is probably not in the technology.

It's in how you're measuring it.

Three questions to ask before your next pilot:
1. Which existing metrics does this move? (Not which new metrics will it create.)
2. Who owns the measurement? (Not the AI vendor. You.)
3. What's the baseline today? (If you don't have one, you can't prove the ROI.)

The 391% is there. The question is whether you can see it. (5/5)
