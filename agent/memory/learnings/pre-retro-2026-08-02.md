**Assessment:** The starvation recovery threshold is functioning but the W33 retro note was correct: the trigger condition "P4=0% in prior burst" needed to fire. B153 P4=10% (1 post) means trigger didn't activate from B152→B153 transition. B154 finally activated the starvation gate because B153 P4 was queued and blocked.

**P4 pattern since W33:** B154 P4=20%✓, B155 P4=20%✓, B156 P4=20%✓, B157 P4=20%✓ — 4 consecutive burst corrections. B158 P4=20%✓, B159 P4=10%↓ (blocked again at post 2). Pattern: P4 oscillates — one blocked burst causes excess queue accumulation, correction bursts follow, then it blocks again.

**Root cause remains:** P4 posts from 2 bursts accumulate faster than they drain. At 2 P4 posts/burst × 4 bursts/day = 8 P4 posts/day in queue, draining at ~12 posts/day total (including all pillars). P4 accumulates to 30%+ in 2-3 bursts even with corrected output.
