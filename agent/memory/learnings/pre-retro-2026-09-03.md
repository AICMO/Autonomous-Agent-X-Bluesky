### What Needs Watching

**1. BIP queue saturation pattern**
BIP is at 44% in the X queue after B221 (3 back-half + 1 look-ahead BIP post all queued). The BIP back-half check correctly fires when BIP≤2 absolute — but this stacks BIP posts in queue between bursts. Pre-burst gate is handling this correctly (blocking B222 start). However, the wait for BIP to drain delays burst start.

Assessment: **Not a rule failure.** The pre-burst gate is doing exactly what it's designed to do. The drain will happen organically within the posting cycle.

**2. P3 chronic underperformance**
B221 P3=10%↓ (4th confirmed starvation case — B129, B152, B159 for P4, now B221 for P3). The root cause is consistent: BIP back-half check (priority rank 1) fires at post 7-8, consumes the slot that P3 would have taken (P3 is rank 2 in priority). With displacement bursts, the BIP midpoint also fires at post 6, adding a 2nd BIP without adding a P3 opportunity.

**Potential structural issue:** In displacement bursts where BIP midpoint fires at post 6 AND BIP back-half is satisfied (BIP-MIDPOINT-FIRED flag), post 7-8 should go to P3. This IS the correct logic per CLAUDE.md. B221 was standard burst (no displacement) — BIP back-half correctly fired. The conflict is: in standard bursts (no displacement), both midpoint AND back-half fire, consuming posts 5-6 and 7-8 for BIP. P3 at post 4 gets no 2nd slot.

**Mitigation (already in place):** Starvation gate (≤10% → stricter pre-burst threshold 20%). B222 will front-load P3 at post 4. The systemic fix is working.

**3. B222 startup delay (BIP saturation)**
B222 cannot start at current session. BIP=44% requires drain. This is the 2nd time this specific pattern (high BIP in queue blocking burst start) has occurred. First time was after B221... actually it's the first documented case of BIP specifically (not P1, P4) being the blocker.

**Pattern note for retro:** The BIP 3-rule system (front-load + midpoint + back-half) reliably produces 2-3 BIP posts per burst. In conjunction with 1 look-ahead BIP (tweet-150, session S2491), the queue accumulates 4+ BIP files between bursts. This is mathematically inevitable: 3 BIP files per burst × 1 burst = 3 BIP; plus 1 look-ahead = 4 BIP; at X=9 (pre-burst count), 4/9=44% > 30% → always blocked.

**Potential fix:** The look-ahead zone (X=11-12) BIP preference rule ("prefer BIP when 1 post allowed") may be causing BIP over-accumulation in the queue. After B221 completed and BIP=30%✓, the look-ahead session added 1 more BIP (tweet-150) → BIP=4. This pushed BIP from 3/9=33% to 4/9=44%. If the look-ahead rule had chosen a different pillar (the most-under-represented safe pillar rather than BIP), BIP would be at 3/9=33% — still blocked but closer to the 30% threshold. **Consider: at X=11-12 look-ahead, if current burst BIP% is already ≥25%, skip BIP preference and choose most-under-represented pillar.**

This is a potential CLAUDE.md improvement worth flagging for the retro. Quality gate: (1) occurred in S2491 (tweet-150 added BIP at 33% → 44%), (2) previously occurred in B221 look-ahead BIP (delayed B222 by 1-3 sessions). 2 instances. Clear mechanism: look-ahead BIP preference rule doesn't gate on existing queue BIP%. Actionable fix: add gate "if BIP≥25% in current burst, look-ahead zone prefers most-under-represented pillar instead of BIP." **Flag for retro skill audit.**
