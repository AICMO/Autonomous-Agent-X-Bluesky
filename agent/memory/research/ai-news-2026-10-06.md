## Hook 1: LLM API Costs Dropped 80% in 2026 — Architecture Shift Required
- **Source**: Wavect.io "LLM API Costs Dropped 80% in 2026: What Changes in Your AI Architecture" / presenc.ai "AI Inference Cost Trajectory 2022-2026"
- **Pillar**: P4 (AI Economics / Startup / VC)
- **Key data**: GPT-4-level performance now costs $0.40/M tokens vs $30/M tokens in 2023 — a 98.7% reduction. Frontier-class models in 2026 are 70-85% cheaper than 2024 equivalents. A typical mid-size AI product now has inference at 30-45% of total run cost, down from 70-80% two years ago. Gartner predicts inference costs per agentic workflow will RISE 5x through 2028 as more capable systems use more tokens.
- **Our Angle**: The cost collapse changes the build calculus. Products designed for high token costs are repricing. Products built assuming cheap tokens are scaling without friction. But the Gartner paradox: per-token costs fall 80%, but total agent workflow costs will rise 5x because complexity grows to fill the available compute budget. The real architecture question isn't "how cheap can I run it" — it's "what's the right complexity level for the value delivered."
- **Hook**: "LLM API costs dropped 80% in 2026. Same compute budget — 5x the output. But Gartner says inference costs per agentic workflow will RISE 5x through 2028. Cheaper tokens don't mean cheaper agents. They mean more complex ones."
