- **Source**: GPUNex (2026), Epoch AI, Report AI token price index
- **Pillar**: P4 (AI Economics)
- **Key data**: GPT-4 equivalent: $20/million tokens in late 2022 → $0.40/million in 2026. That's a 1,000x reduction in ~3.5 years. ## Hook 5: Agentic Marketing Doubles — Real Production Numbers
- **Source**: Chief Content Marketer 2026, The Stacc, Digital Applied
- **Pillar**: P2 (Marketing Automation / Content Ops)
- **Key data**: 34% of enterprise marketing teams run at least one autonomous agent in production — up from 14% in Q4 2025 (2.4x in 10 months). Top deployments: SEO content briefs (58%), campaign analytics summaries (51%), ad copy variants (47%), lead qualification (41%). - **Hook**: "34% of marketing teams now run AI agents in production. Most are automating briefs and summaries. Almost none have removed the human from the actual publish decision. That gap is where the real 4x ROI lives."
