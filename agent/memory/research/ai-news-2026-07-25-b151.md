## P4 — AI Economics / Startup / VC

### Hook A: LLM Price Collapse 94.5% Since 2023
- **Data**: Since March 2023, frontier LLM output prices dropped ~94.5% (price index 100 → 5.5). A capability costing $20/M tokens in late 2022 now costs $0.40.
- **Current (July 2026)**: GPT-5 = $30/M output. DeepSeek V3 = $1.10/M output (frontier capable). Inference cost drop expected to continue at 3-5x/year through 2027 (slowing from 10x/year 2021-2025).
- **Our angle**: When cost drops 94%, quantity demanded explodes. Jevons Paradox in full effect — enterprise AI spend rose 320% in the same window. The savings illusion: cheaper tokens = more tokens consumed. Net spend goes UP.
- **Pillar**: P4 (AI economics, cost structure disruption)
- **Status**: AVAILABLE

### Hook B: AI VC Bubble — $300B Q1 2026 with Cracks Showing
- **Data**: Q1 2026 saw ~$300B in startup investment (up sharply YoY). But: 200+ early-stage AI startups shut down or laid off staff in July 2026. Together AI closed $800M Series C at $8.3B valuation. Goldman Sachs and Altman both warned of bubble dynamics.
- **Our angle**: Valuations running 5-10 years ahead of revenue. Infrastructure bets (compute, pipelines) replacing application layer bets. 5-7 companies absorb most capital → concentration risk. First correction signs appearing while mega-rounds continue.
- **Pillar**: P4 (startup economics, VC dynamics)
- **Status**: AVAILABLE

## P3 — Call Center AI / Voice AI / CX

### Hook A: Voice AI ROI — 45-65% Deflection, 65-90% Cost Reduction
- **Data**: Enterprise contact centers achieve 45-60% Tier-1 call deflection with voice AI. Conversational AI reduces per-interaction cost 65-90% vs live agent. Gartner: conversational AI cuts global CC labor costs $80B by 2026.
- **Caveat**: IrisAgent "Voice AI Benchmarks 2026: Why Most Pilots Miss the 45-65% Mark" — the gap between pilot and production ROI is real and documented.
- **Our angle**: The ROI numbers are real — but only for organizations that complete the transition from pilot to production. 88% of autonomous agent pilots fail before production rollout. The contact center AI ROI story is only for the 12% who make it through.
- **Pillar**: P3 (CC AI ROI, enterprise deployment)
- **Status**: AVAILABLE

### Hook B: 2026 Enterprise CC Deployment Patterns — IVR Replacement as Entry Point
- **Data**: Most 2026 enterprise CC organizations start with Tier 1 IVR replacement (fastest to deploy, most common entry point). SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA, GDPR, EU AI Act compliance now shipped as standard in serious platforms. Voice AI is reaching enterprise compliance grade.
- **Our angle**: Every enterprise that deploys voice AI follows the same staircase: IVR → Tier 1 deflection → AHT reduction → agent augmentation → full automation for simple cases. Ender Turing's data shows where most get stuck (step 2→3).
- **Pillar**: P3 (CC AI deployment patterns, Ender Turing angle)

### Hook A: 29% of AI Agent Deployments Abandoned Within 90 Days (Gartner)

## P1 — Autonomous Agents / Agent Architecture

### Hook A: 95% of Agent Prototypes Never Reach Production
- **Data**: ~95% of enterprise AI agent prototypes never reach production. 88% of autonomous agent pilots fail before production rollout. Gartner predicts 40% of enterprises will demote or decommission agents by 2027 due to governance gaps found only after production incidents.
- **Key finding**: CISA, NSA, and Five Eyes partners published joint guidance May 1, 2026: "autonomous systems are already operating in critical infrastructure with insufficient governance."
- **Our angle**: We've run 1,951+ sessions, 150+ bursts, 3,677 tweets produced autonomously. We're in the 5% that made it to production. The failure mode isn't model quality — it's the 6 surrounding capabilities: governance, observability, hallucination control, cross-framework coordination, multi-cloud reliability, and platform-plus-people know-how.
- **Pillar**: P1 (autonomous agents, production deployment, governance)
- **Status**: AVAILABLE

### Hook B: Gartner — Uniform Governance Across AI Agents Leads to Failure
- **Data**: Gartner (May 26, 2026): "Applying uniform governance across AI agents will lead to enterprise AI agent failure." The distinction: agent's ABILITY to act ≠ scope of ACCESS it should be granted. Governance must match autonomy level.
- **Our angle**: This is a fundamental architectural insight. We built the same way — our agent operates within defined boundaries (agent/config.md), can't delete git history, can't push without review. Graduated trust, not blanket authority. The Gartner finding validates what we learned by building.
- **Pillar**: P1 (agent governance, graduated trust, architecture)
- **Status**: AVAILABLE
