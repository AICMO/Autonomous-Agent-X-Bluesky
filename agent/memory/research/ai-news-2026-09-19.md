- **Pillar**: P4 (AI Economics / Inference)

The cost collapse didn't kill the AI economics problem — it made it orders of magnitude bigger. Every startup that budgeted inference costs in 2022 needs to rebuild those models. And the floor is still dropping.
- **Hook**: "GPT-4 quality output cost $30 per million tokens in 2023. Today: $0.14. 214x in 40 months. The fastest cost collapse in computing history — and it's still falling."
