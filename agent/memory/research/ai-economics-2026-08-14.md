## Inference Cost Collapse (P4)

**Key data points:**
- LLM inference costs dropped 1,000× in 3 years (Nov 2021 → 2024)
- What cost $60/million tokens in Nov 2021 now costs $0.06-0.40 (150-1000x reduction)
- Prices dropped ~80% between early 2025 and early 2026
- OpenAI's o3 fell 80% in a single pricing update
- Future outlook: 3-5x annual reduction through 2027, then tapering to 1.5-2x/year
- Despite this: startups report infrastructure costs consuming 40-60% of revenue

**Angle (P4 / Jevons Paradox):**
Despite 1,000x cost drop, spending keeps rising. The math: costs fell 1,000x → usage rose more than 1,000x → total bill higher. Enterprises cutting costs 30-60% via prompt compression, model tiering, output caching, FinOps governance.

- $407B in enterprise AI spend in 2026 (up 34.8% YoY) — total ~$2.5T worldwide
- Companies expect 171% ROI from agentic AI — but only 39% can point to ANY EBIT impact
- Actual measured returns: Enterprise AI ROI = 21% in 2026 (up from 16% prior year)
- Average investment base = ~$30M. ROI = $6.3M
- Agentic AI projected ROI: $17.6M within 2 years (up from $4.3M estimate last year — 4x jump)
- Agentic AI software: 141% growth to ~$202B
- 23% of orgs already scaling an agentic system; 39% experimenting

**Angle (P4 — ROI reality check):**

**Angle (P4 — Governance = survival):**
40%+ of agentic AI projects canceled by 2027. Not because the tech failed — because nobody could prove it worked. Governance isn't bureaucracy. It's how you survive the next 18 months.
