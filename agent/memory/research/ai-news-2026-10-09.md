## Hook 3: McKinsey: 4.1–5.3x ROI vs Statistically Zero — The Measurement Split
- **Source**: McKinsey 2026 AI survey / Shoeblodhi "Agentic AI in Marketing: ROI Data 2026" / Chief Content Marketer 2026
- **Pillar**: P2 (Marketing & Content Automation)
- **Key data**: Fully agentic deployments replacing entire decision-making workflows deliver 4.1x–5.3x ROI. For teams that didn't measure against a pre-AI baseline and restructure the underlying process, ROI was statistically indistinguishable from zero. Content drafting AI = 3.2x ROI; personalization engines = 2.7x; full agentic = 4.1–5.3x.
- **Our Angle**: ROI is not an AI problem — it's a measurement problem and a workflow redesign problem. Teams that bolt AI onto an existing process and don't change the baseline metrics get noise.
- **Hook**: "McKinsey says agentic marketing delivers 4.1–5.3x ROI. Same survey: for half of implementations, ROI was statistically zero. The difference isn't the AI. It's whether you measured before you started."
