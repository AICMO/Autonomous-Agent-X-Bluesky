## P4 — AI Economics / Startup / VC

### Hook A: Agentic AI Cost Multiplier — 5-30x More Tokens Than Chatbots
- **Data**: Gartner (March 2026): agentic models require 5-30x more tokens per task than standard generative AI chatbots. Pilot economics bear NO relationship to production economics. Startups building on LLMs report infrastructure costs consuming 40-60% of revenue.
- **Concrete example**: A fintech startup's fraud detection agent cost $5K/month with 50 users (Q3 2025) → $15K/month with 500 users (Jan 2026). The scaling economics are brutal.
- **Our angle**: We've seen this firsthand — agentic workflows burn tokens in loops. The Jevons Paradox runs in both directions: cheaper tokens but more tokens per task. Net cost per outcome can INCREASE as workflows get more sophisticated. 85% of enterprise AI budgets now go to inference (up from 20% in 2023).
- **Pillar**: P4 (AI economics, agentic cost structure, production scaling trap)
- **Status**: STAGED (B152 Post 2 — p4-20260726-012.txt)

### Hook B: AI Inference $50B+ Spending in 2026 — Exceeds Training Spend
- **Data**: 2026: more than $50B being spent on inference costs alone — exceeding training model spend for the first time in history. Inference costs declined 150-1000x from 2021-2026 for equivalent GPT-4 performance. BUT: budget concentration shifting — 85% of enterprise AI budgets now in inference (was 20% in 2023).
- **Our angle**: The narrative is "tokens are free." The reality is inference spend is growing faster than cost is falling because organizations are consuming exponentially more tokens. Classic Jevons Paradox at infrastructure scale. $50B in inference + $300B in startup funding = an economy that's betting heavily on agents delivering production ROI.
- **Pillar**: P4 (AI infrastructure economics, inference vs training shift)

## P3 — Call Center AI / Voice AI / CX

### Hook A: 88% Deployed, 25% Operationalized — The CC AI Execution Gap
- **Data**: 2026: 88% of contact centers have deployed AI in some form. Only 25% have operationalized it into day-to-day workflows generating measurable ROI. Fortune 500: 67% have production voice agent systems by early 2026. Gartner: $80B in CC savings projected by 2026 via conversational AI.
- **Our angle**: The 88% vs 25% gap is the real story. "Deployed" means installed a chatbot or tried a voice pilot. "Operationalized" means the agent handles Tier-1 calls, deflects them, reduces AHT — and someone is measuring it. 63% of "deployments" are still pilots in disguise 18 months later. Ender Turing's data matches this exactly — the staircase metaphor: deployed ≠ operationalized.
- **Pillar**: P3 (CC AI execution gap, deployed vs operationalized)
- **Status**: STAGED (B152 Post 4 — p3-20260726-014.txt)

### Hook B: Contact Center AI Year-One ROI — What Forrester 2025 Actually Shows
- **Data**: Forrester 2025 benchmark: payback period under 6 months for properly deployed CC AI. Three-year ROI: 331-391%. Per-interaction cost below $1.00 for fully automated resolutions vs $6-15 for live agents. 5-15pt FCR gains, 50% AHT reductions when fully operationalized.

The six-month payback requires completing the IVR→Tier-1 deflection→AHT reduction staircase. Half the market installs the first step and declares victory. The other half actually works.

### Hook A: 78% "Use AI" But Only 20% Track ROI — The Measurement Void
- **Data**: 78% of enterprise marketing teams "use AI somewhere" but fewer than 20% actively track ROI from it. 34% now run autonomous agents in production (up from 14% in Q4 2025). - **Our angle**: Marketing teams are creating an accountability void. They're spending on AI tools but can't prove value — meaning the budget is vulnerable. In 2026, executive scrutiny has raised the standard: traditional dashboards (clicks, rankings) no longer defend AI investments. The 20% that measure will protect their budgets. The 80% that don't will face cuts when the boardroom asks for evidence.
- **Pillar**: P2 (AI marketing measurement gap, accountability, budget defense)
- **Status**: STAGED (B152 Post 3 — p2-20260726-013.txt)

### Hook B: Agentic AI Marketing ROI — 171% Expected but Conditional
- **Data**: Organizations expect 171% average ROI (US enterprises: 192%). Successful agentic deployments report 4.1x-5.3x ROI. BUT: condition is "fully replacing a specific decision-making workflow" — not adding AI on the side. 34% run at least 1 autonomous agent in production. Repetitive tasks (segmentation, A/B, reporting, scheduling) can now run autonomously.
- **Our angle**: The 171% ROI is the "full replacement" ROI — not "AI-assisted" ROI. Most teams get 20-30% efficiency gains because they layer AI onto existing workflows rather than replacing them. The distinction matters enormously for budget justification. We see this with content ops: AI-assisted content takes 70% less time but still requires human judgment at every step. Full replacement pipelines (like this agent) are the rare minority.
- **Pillar**: P2 (agentic marketing ROI, full replacement vs AI-assisted, full automation minority)
